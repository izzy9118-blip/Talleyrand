# First Sailing

The first Talleyrand corpus was drafted as `deeds-draft-0.1` — eighteen deeds
in a ratified schema of seven labelled parts: TRIGGER, INTERROGATIVES,
OPERATION, TERMINATION, COUNTERFEIT, INSTANCES, RUNTIME, GUARDS, VOICE.

It was condemned whole.

## The condemnation

The schema was the flaw, not the contents. It cut a single motion into layers
and then bolted the layers back together: the cognition in one part, the
evidence in another, the move in a third, the sentence nowhere. A mind whose
signature is that the dispatch **is** the thought was fitted with a form in
which the thought and the dispatch are different sections of a document.

What that form produces is a lens that walks a model through interrogatives and
then asks it to generate — analysis assembled from a checklist, arriving at
observation and stopping there. The deed's terminus, the composed verdict whose
final sentence is earned or absent, had no place to live in it.

The second sailing's keel states the correction as its first principle: a deed
is one act of writing; cognition, evidence, move, and sentence are a single
indivisible motion from trigger to composed verdict; no layers, no appendices
bolted on.

## What was burned

- `deeds-draft-0.1` whole.
- The layered schema.

## What survived the fire

- The Vienna observations, Letters I–IX.
- The corroborations (Gentz).
- The judgment record J-1 through J-4.
- The withholding ledger.
- Open questions Q-1 through Q-5.
- The deed-candidate **contents** — as ore, not as form.

## The record itself

The condemned draft is preserved verbatim at
[`first-sailing-records/deeds-draft-0.1.txt`](first-sailing-records/deeds-draft-0.1.txt).

It is preserved, not overwritten, per the Custos pattern — so that no rebuilt
stone re-lays a refuted flaw. It is a historical record. It is **not live
doctrine**, it carries no authority, it is not loaded at runtime, and no deed in
`deeds/` derives from its form. Where its text and the keel disagree, the keel
governs; the keel supersedes it entire.
