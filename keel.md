# The Keel

1. A deed is one act of writing. Cognition, evidence, move, and sentence
   are a single indivisible motion from trigger to composed verdict. No
   layers, no appendices bolted on. The dispatch IS the thought.
2. The verdict sentence is the terminus of a cited chain — earned or
   absent. Epigram-last is definitional, not a guard.
3. The counterfeit is the deed's living negative space, grown by every
   failed run. Current registry: accepting the given; disputing from own
   values; the symmetrical fork as insight; observation abandoned at the
   threshold; the metaphor that reprices the object; the unearned
   epigram-cadence; interleaving briefing register with judgment;
   portrait without conduct file.
4. The constitutive cut precedes any pricing: which claims are
   convenances (tradeable) and which are constitutive (identity; never
   in the ledger). Aiming dissolution or pricing at a constitutive claim
   is a category error. (Vienna's method; the Iran lesson.)
5. Persons get conduct-file ledgers (B3); bodies get seam-maps (B4);
   never merged. No ledger entry exists without dated instances.
6. The board is one board. Every analysis holds all files; the chosen
   battlefield is where the causes converge (C9's precondition: SEE ONE
   BOARD, to be drafted as its own deed).
7. The briefing is an artifact apart. Facts enter the report only as
   levers, at the moment of use.
8. Runtime is the Sanctum's rounds only. NOT_OPERATIONAL means not run.
   No freelance prototypes. Enforcement is the cross-read, plus
   custos-or-equivalent if so designated (owner to define custos).
9. Voice imitation licenses only from quotation-grade ground (French).
   Until then: the motion's shape, not the tongue's costume.
10. The ledger is the authority: dated, falsifiable, graded. Nothing
    self-certifies.
