# TALLEYRAND — VIENNA CORRESPONDENCE

Working documentary record for deed construction. Nothing in this directory ratifies
a deed or certifies completeness.

## SOURCES

The founding reading began from `TAL-SRC-001`, the OCR of the 1881 English printing.
That witness remains an access copy and is not authoritative for wording.

The repository now also holds:

- `TAL-SRC-005`: the acquired 1881 French Pallain page-image witness. It is the
  quotation-grade target, but exact formulations still require printed-page and
  scan-page pinning before any voice license may issue.
- `TAL-SRC-006`: a French access transcription of the Vienna correspondence in the
  1891 *Mémoires du prince de Talleyrand*, volumes II–III, made available by Project
  Gutenberg from BnF/Gallica source images. It permits the founding reading to
  proceed in French but does not replace TAL-SRC-005 for exact wording.

## STANDING METHOD

- Understand the thing as it is, and as it understands itself, before external
  framing or evaluation.
- Deeds are indexed by capacities of the maker's mind, not by events.
- The letters are the window; the deeds and flow of action are the object.
- Analysis that terminates in observation is a broken Talleyrand. The finding must
  reach the actionable point or remain a finding rather than a deed.
- The inverted lens is now active: every episode is read both for judgment and for
  how information moved—source, channel, access, corroboration, disclosure timing,
  audience, and the consequences of each.
- Irony is not mechanical inversion. Preserve uncertainty. No self-certification.

## STATE AFTER LETTER XXV

The extended reading condition imposed by the founding deed index has been met.
The result was discriminating rather than confirmatory:

- A4 and B7 materially thickened to draftable candidates.
- C5 materially thickened, but its decisive Alexander evidence remains `[HT]`.
- A6 remains thin.
- B1 remains dependent on the Letter IX counter-construction.
- B5 requires the missing discovery protocol before safe promotion.
- C6 and C10 remain substantially self-testimonial.
- C9 requires a redundancy test rather than automatic promotion.
- D1 failed its current formulation because the extended correspondence strengthens
  the evidence for Louis XVIII as an independently reasoning sovereign principal.

The reading also established that information logistics are too foundational to
remain scattered observations. The next constitutional production unit is the
missing **Talleyrand discovery protocol**: a committed rule for source provenance,
channel quality, access, corroboration, disclosure timing, audience, and the
boundary between documented suppression and ordinary silence.

After that protocol is committed, deed drafting/retirement can proceed against the
now-expanded ground, followed by owner per-deed rulings, page-pinned voice review,
and only then the sovereign minister interface.
