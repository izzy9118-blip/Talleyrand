# PART FOUR — OPEN QUESTIONS

Q-1. **THE ROYAL BACK-CHANNEL.** Tertiary sources state Louis XVIII distrusted
Talleyrand and negotiated with Metternich by mail. Unverified. If true, it re-reads
the entire correspondence. High consequence. [WH]

**Letters X-XXV test:** NO RESOLUTION. The extended correspondence makes independent
royal agency unmistakable but does not itself prove a separate Louis–Metternich
back-channel. Keep open and seek the actual correspondence rather than repeating the
tertiary claim.

Q-2. **PATERNITY OF DOCTRINE.** The King is no instrument: he states method,
produces his own strategic survey, and states the non-sanction doctrine in dynastic
key. Who taught whom cannot be assumed. [UU]

**Letters X-XXV test:** STRENGTHENED AS AN UNRESOLVED LIMIT. Louis independently
authorizes, refuses, orders military preparation, judges ministers and sovereigns,
approves the 3 January convention, and supplies reasons in his own register. The
later evidence makes D1's old 'bind the principal by attribution' formulation less
credible, not more. Any principal-side deed must preserve reciprocal authorization
and the King's sovereign judgment. Do not assign doctrinal paternity to Talleyrand.

Q-3. **AUDIENCE LAYERING.** The dispatches have several readers. Whether they carry
a surface for the court and a teaching for the King alone was unsettled. [UU]

**Letters X-XXV test:** PARTIALLY RESOLVED / NARROWED. Intentional multi-audience
construction is documented: Letter XVII describes a diplomatic note built for
possible reading beyond its immediate recipient, and Letter XXIII constructs a
facts-only public account designed to produce reflection in the reader. What remains
unproved is the stronger claim that the royal dispatches themselves contain a
special court-surface over a concealed King-only teaching. Preserve that narrower
question.

Q-4. **THE INFORMANT IN LETTER IX.** Whether the source is wholly real, or partly a
device for distancing the analysis's cynicism from himself, cannot be settled from
the text. [UU]

**Letters X-XXV test:** UNRESOLVED. The extended block demonstrates careful use of
confidential intermediaries and source-channel distinctions, which makes a real
informant entirely plausible; it does not identify or independently verify the
Letter IX source. Do not convert plausibility into verification.

Q-5. **HIS MODEL OF THE FRENCH REVOLUTION.** Never systematically stated; fragments
surface under argumentative load. [UU]

**Letters X-XXV test:** STILL FRAGMENTARY. Later letters continue to invoke
revolutionary/Jacobin conditions tactically, especially in Italy, but do not supply
a reconstructed model adequate for a deed. Continue collection; keep low weight.
