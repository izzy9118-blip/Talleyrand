# PART SIX — CORROBORATION STATUS

  CORROBORATED: the 30 September conference (Gentz's diary — plans upset,
  two hours, unforgettable; Labrador's line in both accounts).
  EXTERNALLY SUPPORTED: the candor strategy's effect (Bavarian surveillance
  via Noailles); Castlereagh's regret at not convening the Congress
  (department letter, 24 Oct).
  UNCORROBORATED AND HIGH VALUE: Jacobi-Kloest; both Alexander audiences;
  the Letter IX counter-construction.
  WITNESSES TO PURSUE: Gentz (verified online); Castlereagh's correspondence
  vol. 10 (present); Metternich's memoirs (present); then Muenster,
  d'Angeberg's documents, Jaucourt, Wellington's supplementary despatches,
  Nesselrode.
  NOT YET ACQUIRED AND MOST WANTED: the letters to the Duchesse de Courlande
  — the private register of the same weeks. Pairing King's dispatch /
  Courland letter / Gentz diary would show the mind in three lights.
