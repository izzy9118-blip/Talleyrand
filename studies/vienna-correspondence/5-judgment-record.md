# PART FIVE — JUDGMENT RECORD (DATED, TESTABLE)

  J-1. ELBA. 13 Oct 1814: pay what Fontainebleau owes; move Bonaparte to the
  Azores. The King assents in principle (21 Oct) and privately offers to
  exceed the treaty's terms to get the distance. Neither executed. March
  1815 follows from the two failures — grievance and proximity.
  VERDICT: correct, disregarded, priced.

  J-2. THE LEAGUE'S LEAK. 13 Oct: the union is apparent, not real, held
  together only by disbelief in French means and will. 17 Oct: Castlereagh
  shows him England's Memorandum.
  VERDICT: diagnosis dated, event dated, correct.

  J-3. GERMANY. 17 Oct: revolutionary ferment; the universities and their
  young men; unity as fanatical religion infecting even princes; German
  unity harmless to a France holding the left bank and Belgium, grave to the
  France of today.
  CONFIRMATIONS: Burschenschaften 1815; Wartburg 1817; Kotzebue 1819;
  Carlsbad Decrees 1819; and the strategic clause vindicated in 1870 — which
  is also why Pallain published in 1881, a curatorial fact to hold in view.
  ERROR: the carrier class. Judged by 1848 he is wrong (the revolution was
  bourgeois and professorial). Judged by 1866-71 the error inverts: unity
  arrived from above, crown-led, exactly the shape his frame allowed.
  METHOD: comparative, parameterized, variance-aware; refuses false
  precision ("who can say where the impulse, once given, might stop");
  prices the horizon ("during the whole of your Majesty's reign").
  CAUTION: the dangers selected are the ones his brief needs. Not
  falsified — but the selection is his signature, and the omission of
  France's own hand in the dissolution belongs to the withholding ledger.

  J-4. ENGLISH CONVERSION. Worked for from 29 Sept (inspire him with
  firmness) through 4 Oct (the responsibility lever) to 17 Oct (the
  Memorandum shown).
  [ED-PRIMARY] 3 Jan 1815 to Jaucourt: the English embassy "has changed
  entirely, and is now proceeding in harmony with our views."
  VERDICT: sought, achieved, dated.
