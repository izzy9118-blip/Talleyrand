# PART THREE — THE WITHHOLDING LEDGER

What the dispatches do not contain. Tested as witnesses fill in.

  - NO FAILURES. He loses no scene in nine letters.
  - NO PRICES. What accommodation costs is absent; what it gains is recorded.
  - NO MONEY. His documented venality across three decades has no register
    here. (Note the Bavarian surveillance report: no money spent at Vienna.
    The silence may here be truthful.)
  - NO NETWORK. The private channels appear only as anonymized sources ("a
    man whose position..."), veiled even from the King.
  - NO SELF. No doubt, no fear, no precariousness — though Letter XI will
    break this twice, the first counter-instances.
  - NO SWEAT. Duration and vehemence removed; two hours become surgical
    touches. Established by the Gentz divergence.
  - HIS OWN HAND IN WHAT HE DEPLORES. Analyzing the consequences of the
    dissolution of the Germanic Empire, he does not mention that the
    Confederation of the Rhine was Napoleon's charter, negotiated in part by
    his own ministry. His history surfaces once in nine letters, and
    surfaces as a vagueness ("under different circumstances").
  - THE VIENNA HE LIVED IN. No Kaunitz palace, no salon, no Duchess. Pallain
    exposes the silence accidentally through a genealogical footnote.
