# POST-DISCOVERY CANDIDATE DISPOSITIONS

This record applies the independent-source probe to candidates whose status depended
materially on contested or self-testimonial claims. It does not ratify or certify a deed.

## C5 — BUILD THE DOOR FOR HIS WORD

**Disposition:** `THICKENED_HT_REMAINS`.

The 17 November Alexander audience still provides a strong example of constructing
an honorable route out of a prior commitment, but the decisive dialogue remains
Talleyrand's account of his own conduct. This probe located no independent contemporary
witness covering the material exchange. `SEARCHED_NOT_FOUND` does not count as confirmation.

**Consequence:** preserve as a candidate. Do not owner-ratify from this ground alone.

## C6 — PRE-LOAD THE CHANNEL, READ THE GREETING

**Disposition:** `THICKENED_HT_REMAINS`.

The extended correspondence independently thickens the general importance of channel
selection and access, but the decisive greeting/audience episode remains self-testimonial.

**Consequence:** preserve as a candidate; channel mechanics belong securely in the
ratifiable discovery protocol even if this specific deed never clears its evidence bar.

## B1 — RECOVER THE DESIGN FROM FRAGMENTS

**Disposition:** `PARTIALLY_CORROBORATED_BOUNDED_ONLY`.

Independent British documentary ground supports narrower pieces of the Letter IX picture:
British policy was actively managing French influence, Wellington was used as a separate
channel, and Castlereagh did not initially treat Talleyrand as an unrestricted confidential
partner. That does not independently establish the anonymous informant's entire design or
its claimed authorship and purposes.

**Consequence:** B1 may survive only as a bounded reconstruction rule with native
probability typing. It may not treat the Letter IX grand design as documented fact.

## C10 — CONVERT THE ALLY'S FRUSTRATION

**Disposition:** `RETIRE_ONE_WAY_CONVERSION_FORMULATION`.

The later Franco-British-Austrian alignment is documented. The causal story is not one-way.
Talleyrand's later dispatch gives him an important causal role; Castlereagh's own 21 November
account gives Wellington an important role in rendering French influence more accommodating
and describes Talleyrand as becoming less obstructive. These are independent participant
accounts and materially disagree about direction of influence.

**Consequence:** the current deed candidate cannot be promoted. A future replacement, if
needed, must model **reciprocal alignment through multiple channels** and preserve competing
participant causal accounts rather than awarding the conversion to one actor.
