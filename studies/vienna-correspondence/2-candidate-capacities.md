# PART TWO — CANDIDATE CAPACITIES

Candidates only. Not deeds. Naming and consolidation still open.

C-1. THE SURFACE IS THE PATH.
  The governing capacity, and the one that must not be reduced to
  perception. Perception, judgment, and action are fused: the detail is
  selected because acting there reaches the heart of the matter. In a world
  of ceremony and form, precedence IS position, diction IS claim, a guest
  list IS a map of the possible — so force applied at the surface arrives at
  the center. Crucially this is the weak power's only available lever:
  others act at the scale of armies and provinces; he acts at the scale of
  prepositions, chairs, tables and minutes, instruments no one can deny him.
  Instances: "assist"/"met" rewritten; the vacant seat taken; "allies"
  challenged and the protocol killed; the two dates; Metternich's
  involuntary surprise read as proof of the secrecy pact; the twenty minutes
  counted; which fête can be attended; the horses and the disordered
  precedence.
  BAR: analysis that terminates in observation is a broken Talleyrand. The
  signature is termination in the actionable point.
  NOTE ON GRAIN: for a persecuted writer, surface is constructed to
  misdirect and one reads AGAINST it. Here surface is largely involuntary —
  faces, seating, timing — and one reads WITH it, because the involuntary
  cannot lie. Same rigor, opposite grain.

C-2. THE FIXED FACT AS SOLVENT.
  Dissolving the adversary's given with a fact he cannot refuse: his own
  treaty, date, signature, right. Traps assembled from the victim's own
  commitments, so that resisting means contradicting oneself in public.
  Instances: the treaty date annihilating the interval; "allies" tested
  against the state of relations; the guarantee collapsed (ought-not
  therefore could-not); Jacobi confronted with Prussia's own cessions.
  REFINEMENT: works even on facts aimed at France, provided another hand can
  be induced to apply it — Castlereagh disarming the 1813 clause unprompted,
  with clause and gloss recorded together.

C-3. SEAM LOCATION.
  In every court, coalition and man, the first thing located is the crack;
  then it is widened. Alexander vs. his ministers; Stadion displaced and
  therefore communicative; Castlereagh split from the continentals by his
  parliamentary responsibility; the English mission split from the Prince
  Regent's own views; Alexander split from himself by the offer of a better
  self. Corollary: the displaced or aggrieved man is the intelligence
  source.

C-4. CHOICE OF BATTLEFIELD — DOCUMENTS.
  Forcing negotiation onto paper, where signatures bind and conversations
  evaporate; refusing untraced exchanges; the signed note as instrument.
  ("If it goes well without saying, it will go still better by saying it" —
  [V], preserved in French by Pallain.)

C-5. PRE-AUTHORIZATION GRAMMAR.
  Policy advanced as hope, incredulity, or premature proposal, so that the
  principal's silence licenses it; weapons drafted, sheathed and banked for
  later use. Instances: the intent to contest Russian policy in Alexander's
  presence; the unanimous-declaration float; the manifesto reserved to
  "recur to hereafter"; the war-powers request scripted around a future
  question not yet asked.

C-6. COALITION READ AS SHARED DISBELIEF.
  An adversary league held together by beliefs about one's means and will,
  and therefore dissoluble by managed perception. Verified by events: the
  league leaked exactly where he said it was weakest.

C-7. BURIAL BY POSITION.
  The largest move filed in the smallest place: readmission as housekeeping;
  Saxony as arithmetic; leverage intelligence as gossip.

C-8. ASSESSMENT LEDGER, THEN OPERATION.
  Persons assessed as material in one letter and operated on in the next.
  Castlereagh's timidity (29 Sept) becomes the lever (4 Oct). Ledger entries
  are defects, and the map of allies is drawn mostly in defects.

C-9. NATIVE EVIDENCE TYPING.
  Distinguishes witnessed fact from construction inside his own dispatches:
  "although neither one nor the other has told me so"; "without affirming
  that all this information is perfectly exact... extremely probable."
  Corpus grounding for the evidence-bound speech guard.

C-10. TEMPO AND THE THIRD PLAYER.
  Prices time where others treat it as neutral; reads the population behind
  the plenipotentiaries; derives requirements (accelerate, end) from social
  conditions rather than from the negotiating table. Deterrence priced at
  the smallest sufficient quantum: the hint.

C-11. THEATER WITH ACCOUNT BOOKS.
  Comic, tragic and ceremonial registers played and read; every performance
  extracts something specific (a withdrawn protocol, a naked declaration);
  effects measured and rationed in the moment; the mechanism then disclosed
  candidly to the principal.
