# CANDIDATE TEST — LETTERS X THROUGH XXV

The founding rule required the thin and self-testimonial candidates to meet more
Vienna ground before drafting or owner ratification. This file records the result.
It does not ratify a deed.

## Candidates materially strengthened

### A4 — READ THE DOCUMENT BY ITS TRUE AUDIENCE

**Prior state:** [T][HT]

**Test result:** THICKENED_TO_DRAFTABLE, owner ruling still required.

Letter XVII supplies an explicit multi-audience construction: Talleyrand describes
his note to Metternich as an instrument designed not merely for Metternich but for
possible reading in England and France, with wording selected for particular
purposes. Letter XXIII adds a public narrative deliberately limited to facts so the
reader's own inference bears the effect. This is no longer dependent on the single
Letter IX reading of Castlereagh's memorandum.

**Limit:** Q3 is not thereby closed. Deliberate multi-audience construction is
documented; the stronger hypothesis that the royal dispatches themselves contain a
special court-surface and a King-only teaching layer remains unresolved.

### B7 — REFUSE THE FALSE SCALE

**Prior state:** [T]

**Test result:** THICKENED_TO_DRAFTABLE, owner ruling still required.

Letter XIV attacks a policy that treats political masses without examining their
constituent elements. Letter XVIII refuses population headcount as a sufficient
measure and insists on differences in land, capital, productive condition, and kind.
Letters XXII–XXIV recruit military/topographic expertise when territorial arithmetic
alone cannot answer the security question.

**Sharpening:** the deed should not mean merely 'reject analogies.' It should mean:
identify the parameter that gives the aggregate its causal force; if the composition
differs, refuse the aggregate as the governing scale and recruit the ground needed
to price the actual elements.

### C5 — BUILD THE DOOR FOR HIS WORD

**Prior state:** [T][HT]

**Test result:** THICKENED, BUT SELF-TESTIMONY LIMIT REMAINS.

Letter XII supplies a clearer second form of the operation: Alexander's promise to
Prussia is treated as a constraint that requires an honorable route of retreat;
Talleyrand proposes satisfying Prussia elsewhere so Alexander can change the Saxon
outcome without confessing a dishonorable breach. Letter XXI and Letter XXV add the
general pattern that prior public commitments become prisons and that retreat often
requires a face-saving route.

**Limit:** the decisive Alexander audience remains Talleyrand's own report. Drafting
may now be warranted, but ratification should preserve the [HT] limit unless an
external witness independently confirms the exchange.

## Candidates strengthened but not cleared

### A6 — READ THE PERIPHERY AS GAUGE

**Result:** THICKENED_SLIGHTLY, STILL THIN.

Smaller German states, Swiss actors, and public opinion repeatedly register pressure
before the great-power settlement moves. But the evidence still does not isolate a
stable operation distinct enough from A5 (ground beneath the table) and B4 (locate
the seam). Do not draft separately yet.

### B1 — RECOVER THE DESIGN FROM FRAGMENTS

**Result:** STILL [HT].

The later letters repeatedly validate the practice of assembling plans from channels,
appointments, notes, and behavior, but they do not independently corroborate the
Letter IX counter-construction that grounds the candidate. Preserve; do not upgrade.

### B5 — MODEL THE SECOND ORDER

**Result:** THICKENED_BUT_NOT_SEPARATED.

Letters XIII–XVI provide more examples of asking what a stated complaint, report, or
position is doing beyond its surface claim. The material strengthens the capacity,
but it still overlaps B1 and B4 and remains prone to speculative overreach. Keep as a
working candidate until the discovery protocol supplies a harder evidence boundary.

### C6 — PRE-LOAD THE CHANNEL, READ THE GREETING

**Result:** THICKENED_BUT_[HT]_REMAINS.

Letters X–XVI materially deepen channel selection: which person has access, which
intermediary transmits quickly, which channel is degraded, and how visibility of a
meeting changes its meaning. The original decisive greeting evidence remains his own
report of the Alexander audience. Preserve without ratification for now.

### C9 — FIGHT WHERE THE CAUSES CONVERGE

**Result:** THICKENED, DISTINCTNESS STILL OPEN.

The Saxon campaign ultimately aligns balance, legitimacy, German opinion, Austrian
security, British interest, and French reintegration. Yet the extended block also
shows that this may be the compound result of B4, B6, C1, and C11 rather than a
separate capacity. Test for redundancy before drafting.

### C10 — CONVERT THE ALLY'S FRUSTRATION

**Result:** THICKENED_BUT_[HT]_REMAINS.

Castlereagh's movement from wartime coalition habits to signed alignment is now
traceable across several letters. Talleyrand gives a causal account of the conversion,
but much of the mechanism is still his own retrospective dispatch testimony. Preserve
as candidate; do not certify the causal story merely because the treaty exists.

## Candidate that failed its current formulation

### D1 — BIND THE PRINCIPAL BY ATTRIBUTION

**Prior state:** [T — CAUTION]

**Test result:** DO_NOT_DRAFT_AS_WRITTEN.

The extended correspondence increases rather than resolves Q2. Louis XVIII supplies
independent strategic reasoning, gives and withholds authorization, orders military
preparation, states principles in his own terms, ratifies the 3 January convention,
and sometimes reaches the same practical conclusion before receiving Talleyrand's
next report. The evidence does not support treating the King as a passive principal
whose doctrine is manufactured by attribution.

**Disposition:** retire the current formulation from the draft queue. If a principal-
side deed is later written, it must model **reciprocal authorization and doctrinal
alignment under a sovereign principal**, not psychological capture of the principal
by attribution.

## New material that belongs to existing safeguards before new deeds

### INFORMATION LOGISTICS

Letters X–XXV repeatedly distinguish direct witness, rumor, confidential document,
independent repetition, access to a principal, intermediary reliability, source
location, disclosure timing, and audience. This is too foundational to hide inside a
single 'intelligence' deed. It should become the missing committed discovery protocol
that the founding record already identifies as absent.

### CONSTITUTIVE CUT BEFORE PRICING

Letters XII and XVI refuse to turn Saxony, Naples, alliance, and marriage into one
market merely because another actor proposes a bundle. This is strong new ground for
the existing safeguard `constitutive_cut_precedes_pricing`; do not create a redundant
deed unless later material shows a distinct operation.
