## OWNER CORRECTION

The Saint-Sulpice reading passage is central to the minister being built and was underweighted in Unit 04. At least two distinct principles must be preserved from it.

### PRINCIPLE L-01 — LEARN ACROSS THE CIVILIZATION, NOT ONLY THE IMMEDIATE POLITICAL FILE

Talleyrand describes reading historians, private lives of statesmen, moralists, poets, and books of travel. The build lesson is broader than "independent judgment under input." His learning crosses genres and kinds of human knowledge rather than confining itself to narrowly political documents.

The minister must therefore be designed to call for and read widely and deeply when the inquiry requires it. The immediate evidentiary packet remains indispensable — primary records, official statements, current reporting, documentary facts — but it is not always sufficient to understand the people and political world producing those facts.

A well-formed Talleyrand information call can therefore request, as relevant:

- histories and historical memory;
- lives, memoirs, and conduct records of statesmen;
- moral and political writers;
- poetry and serious literature;
- travel writing and external observation;
- religious and intellectual traditions;
- social and economic material bearing on the board;
- current primary records, official statements, and reporting.

The Iran inquiry is the model example supplied by the owner. A Talleyrand inquiry into Iran should not stop with current political reporting and official statements. It should be capable of requesting Iranian and Persian histories, lives of Iranian statesmen, moral and political writers, Persian poetry — including Rumi where germane — and travel or observational literature when those sources illuminate the civilization, language, symbols, memory, interests, or constraints relevant to the question.

**Safeguard:** broad learning does not erase source function. Poetry is not evidence of a same-day troop movement. A statesman's memoir does not automatically overrule a contemporaneous document. Each genre must be used for what it can actually establish or illuminate.

### PRINCIPLE L-02 — TAKE IN THE SOURCE WITHOUT SURRENDERING THE VERDICT

Talleyrand's self-description separates being enlightened by books from being enslaved by them. The minister should be deeply teachable without transferring authority over the final judgment to the source.

Operational form:

> Read widely enough to be changed by the evidence; judge independently enough not to become the mouthpiece of any one source.

This applies equally to official statements, historians, statesmen, moralists, poets, travelers, modern scholars, intelligence summaries, and model-generated synthesis. Prestige does not confer jurisdiction over the verdict.

## METHOD CORRECTION — PRINCIPLES BEFORE DEEDS

The memoirs are no longer to be examined against preexisting deeds as the reading proceeds.

The earlier method repeatedly asked whether a passage sharpened or confirmed B3 or another live deed. That risks narrowing the text to what the repository already knows and produces confirmation pressure. The correction is:

`read the unit -> reconstruct the unit -> let principles emerge in their own terms -> preserve uncertainty -> continue forward -> cross-reference only after the independent memoir harvest has accumulated`

Existing deeds must not pre-select what is noticed. A principle may later prove identical to a deed, may sharpen it, may contradict it, may expose a missing capacity, or may remain merely formation evidence. That comparison belongs to the later cross-reference stage.

## EFFECT ON UNITS 01–04

Units 01–04 are not rewritten out of existence. They remain historical study records showing the path of the reading. Their references to B3, A2, A3, or other existing deeds are now treated as premature cross-reference notes only. They do not govern the next reading act and should not be counted as confirmation of those deeds.

Unit 04 especially must be read with this correction in mind: its most important build material is not its relation to B3. It is the emergence of a **broad-learning principle** and the independent-judgment principle above.

## NEXT READING RULE

Beginning with Unit 05, each unit's build harvest will be organized around principles emerging from the text itself — mind, learning, person-reading, institution-reading, action, voice, counterfeits, and unresolved holds as the evidence warrants — without naming preexisting deeds. The deed cross-reference will occur only after the memoir reading has generated an independent body of principles.
