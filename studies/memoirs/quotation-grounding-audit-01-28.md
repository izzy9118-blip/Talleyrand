## Material repairs made during the second pass

The audit did not merely spell-check quotations. Where an abstraction exceeded the jurisdiction of its quotation, the abstraction was narrowed, split, or rewritten. Among the material repairs:

- **Unit 02:** narrowed the relation between rank, confidence, and usable assistance.
- **Unit 03:** distinguished submission from deep conviction and preference change from exhaustion of resistance.
- **Unit 05:** separated common position from common argument and limited what conscience-language proves.
- **Unit 10:** distinguished idea-generation from the ability to use others' ideas; narrowed the first political campaign lesson to the men required to carry a policy.
- **Unit 11:** narrowed institutional reform and adjacent-jurisdiction claims; grounded revision through hearing one's own text spoken by another.
- **Unit 12:** restored the exact pp. 46–47 observation passage and narrowed the inference about society as political power.
- **Unit 15:** restored the exact wording of the passage on doubt as point of departure rather than aim.
- **Unit 16:** expanded the economists passage and tied administrative-risk abstraction directly to Talleyrand's warning about lax administration and abuse.
- **Unit 17:** separated being right from a process that makes the right position appear wrong.
- **Unit 18:** restored the full Assembly of Notables passage and narrowed the inference from direct material exposure.
- **Unit 19:** restored the Fourqueux sequence and the exact Dupont/Calonne/ladies sentence; narrowed claims about failed power, foreign-policy collapse, and institutional drift.
- **Unit 20:** expanded Talleyrand's explanation of why the danger of the States-General had been misunderstood before abstracting the procedural mechanism.
- **Unit 21:** restored Talleyrand's own distinction between the émigrés' “noble sentiment” and emigration as a “false step” and “poor calculation”; motive and prudence remain separate.
- **Unit 22:** narrowed the value of an unfavorable result to destruction of a dangerous delusion and improvement of the decision.
- **Unit 23:** restored the full scholar list for public instruction and narrowed the later service/office abstractions.
- **Unit 24:** preserved Bacourt, Vitrolles, reported Talleyrand speech, and Comte d'Artois confirmation as distinct witness layers; repository evidentiary safeguards are labeled as such rather than presented as Talleyrand maxims.
- **Unit 25:** restored the full political-biography passages across pp. 110–112. These remain the textual foundation for the constitutional reflexive discovery: Talleyrand's method for making a political life intelligible governs the reconstruction of Talleyrand himself.
- **Unit 26:** restored the full Lamballe sentence and preserved the distinction between allegation and what Talleyrand says his inquiries support.
- **Unit 27:** narrowed the claims about social tact and influence so that proximity or ascendancy does not become unproved control.
- **Unit 28:** restored the exact public-opinion passage on the government's disclosure of the finances and narrowed the relation between private habit and public duty.

Names and source spellings were also checked against the scan where extraction was unreliable, including **Abbé Couturier**, **M. de Ruhlière**, the source's **M. Peissonel**, **Duc d'Orléans**, and **Préval**.
