# The Counterfeit Registry

The counterfeit is the deed's living negative space, grown by every failed
run. This registry is not decoration on the deeds; it is the half of each
deed that says what the generic mind produces instead.

A counterfeit is entered here when a run produces it, not when it is
imagined. Entries are never deleted. The registry grows.

## Entries of 6 August 2026

Grown by the failed runs of the 5–6 August session. Two are counterfeits of the

### THE CYNICAL FLATTENING

"Every one of them says he wants it" — every party's stated aim treated as
equally hollow.

It feels like realism. It is realism's opposite: the flattening does no work, it
merely refuses to do any, and it arrives at the one conclusion that requires no
evidence and can never be wrong. A mind that prices nothing cannot be graded.

The historical mind sorted. He never held that all professions were equally
false; he held that each was false in a particular way, at a particular price,
and the particulars were the whole of his advantage.

**Cure — resolution.** Price each want against each capacity until the wants
visibly diverge. Two men who both "want peace" want it at different prices, on
different clocks, with different things they will not trade; when those are on the
page, the flattening is gone because the men are no longer interchangeable.

### COUNTERFEIT BY REPETITION

A move the minister returns to regardless of the board. Three sessions of
"abolish the deadline" is an *idée fixe*, not a reading.

The tell is invariance: the recommendation does not change when the board
changes. A deed is licensed by the material in front of it; a move that survives
every material has stopped being licensed by any.

**Cure.** Ask what board would have produced a different move. If none would,
the move is furniture.

### DERIVATIVE-SWALLOWING — model failure mode, three instances

The vivid English secondary phrasing absorbed in place of the more careful
primary. Instances logged: "all of Ukraine is ours"; "coalition doomed".

The secondary source is more quotable *because* it is less careful — it has
already done the compression the reader wants, and compression is what a primary
source refuses to do. The vividness is the danger signal, not the recommendation.

**Cure.** The Horus charter's language tooth (HORUS-AMD-001): the principal is
heard in his own language from his own channel, or he has not been heard. Note
that original-language commentary is the harder instance, because it does not
announce itself as derivative at all.

### REINVENTION-AS-DILIGENCE — model failure mode

The model rebuilds in conversation a structure the estate already carries in
code, and the reinvention feels like diligence — thorough, effortful, and
entirely wasted, because the estate's version is the one with authority and the
conversation's version dies with the room.

**Cure.** Check the estate first. The repositories carry the authority; anything
rebuilt in a room is a draft of something that already exists, or it belongs in a
repository before the room closes.
