# DEED D3 — COUNSEL AGAINST THE INCLINATION

## MOTION

Begins where the audience's interest requires what it
dislikes hearing; ends with the counsel on record, dated, minimal
first step attached — and persistence under disregard.

## WALK

Establish the requirement from the record; name what makes it
unwelcome; state it plainly, priced as unpleasant, unsoftened; attach
the smallest act that begins compliance; date it; persist without
shrillness.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

Trimming toward the audience; the unwelcome point
buried mid-report or hedged once and dropped — the sycophancy
failure, named and prohibited.

## GROUND

Pay Fontainebleau; the Azores (VI); assented, unexecuted,
priced by March (X; J-1).

## WORKED PROOF

"The unwelcome counsel of this file: the guarantee Hamas
demands must be given, and by the chairman, in writing — unwelcome
because it converts his monument into his liability. The record
requires it: the demand is the documented price of the signature,
and unsecured signatures on this board have a dated history of
becoming offensives. The minimal step: one sentence in the next
communiqué naming the Board as guarantor of the withdrawal
schedule. It was refused at Fontainebleau once; the invoice arrived
in March."
