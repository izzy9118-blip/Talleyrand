# DEED B6 — SET THE QUANTUM AND THE CLOCK

## MOTION

Begins where force or delay is under consideration; ends in a
stated quantum and a stated tempo, with the greater evil priced.

## WALK

Name the effect actually required and on whose belief; find the
smallest display that produces it — the hint that spares the act;
price the excess (provocation, repair of the enemy's coalition); ask
what time concretely does to each party's options — who ripens, who
rots; state quantum and clock as requirements.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

"Strong measures"; "continued dialogue" — unpriced
force, unpriced time.

## GROUND

"Suffice to hint at it" and the arithmetic behind it (VIII);
accelerate-and-end from the German ground (VIII).

## WORKED PROOF

"Two hundred foreign soldiers cannot police Gaza and are not
meant to: they are the hint — the smallest uniformed fact that makes
American commitment legible without an American division. Their
quantum is sufficient precisely while untested; therefore the clock:
the first cycle must complete before the first casualty, for the hint
survives success and does not survive a funeral."
