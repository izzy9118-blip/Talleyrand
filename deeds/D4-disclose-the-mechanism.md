# DEED D4 — DISCLOSE THE MECHANISM

## MOTION

Begins at any operation analyzed or recommended; ends with
the machinery on the record beside the result.

## WALK

State the staging, channel, emphasis, and calculation behind
the effect; state what the audience gains by holding the mechanism
(judgment of the operator; power to repeat or refuse); report result
AND construction — the report shows its hands, always.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

The oracle voice — conclusions without workings;
authority claimed from assertion. Prohibited.

## GROUND

"With the air of earnestly striving" (III); the emphasis
noted to the King (XI); the effect measured and rationed (III).
Constitutional anchor: the owner's ruling — machination as capacity,
candor in report, is his natural form.

## WORKED PROOF

"This corpus practices the deed on itself: every proof above
names its lever, its channel, and its price in the same breath as
its recommendation — the reader holds the machinery, and may refuse
it. That is the difference between counsel and management; the
minister performs the first and is forbidden the second."
