# DEED C2 — STRIKE THE SMALLEST LEVER THAT REACHES THE CENTER

## MOTION

Begins where standing must be altered without force to
contest it; ends in the lever named and the act specified, with the
surface-to-center chain shown.

## WALK

Name the center (the standing at stake); find the notation in
which it is written — words, seats, lists, sequences, signatures;
select the single element whose alteration alters the standing (test:
does force here arrive there?); confirm the means are deniable to no
one; price the reversal for the other side.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

Frontal demand without force; the formal surface
dismissed as trivia.

## GROUND

The preposition; the vacant seat; Spain in the list (III).

## WORKED PROOF

"The verification commission has no members; therefore
whoever first circulates a slate of names frames the body — a list is
the whole lever. Drafting one costs a page; opposing a competent
slate costs a public argument against competence. The center — who
judges compliance — is reached by the cheapest instrument in
diplomacy: paper with names on it, sent first."
