# DEED B3 — ASSESS THE PERSON AS MATERIAL

## MOTION

Begins at first contact with a principal; ends in a ledger
entry with an operational assignment — and no entry exists without a
conduct file of dated instances. The verdict sentence is the LAST line
of the chain or it is not written.

## WALK

Build the conduct file first: documented acts, dated. From it:
what he controls and what controls him; what he fears and wishes to be
thought; the working defect; the assignment (channel, lever, obstacle,
instrument); what would revise the entry.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

The balanced pen-portrait assigning nothing;
moralizing the defect; the portrait preceding the file (the Netanyahu
failure, logged).

## GROUND

Castlereagh: timidity documented (II), consumed (III).
Alexander: glory documented (I), addressed (audience). Counter-ground:
the withdrawn Netanyahu epigram — cadence without chain.

## WORKED PROOF

"Entry: the chairman of the Board. Conduct file: chairs the
body himself rather than appointing (Jan 2026); announces in
superlatives on his own channel (30 Jul); office states timing must
yield to 'getting it right' while he states completion (30 Jul).
Assignment: proprietor — the deal is a monument bearing his name;
therefore the guarantee Hamas demands is purchasable from him as a
feature of the monument, and threats to the monument's appearance
outrank threats to its substance. Revise if he delegates the chair."

## AMENDMENT PENDING — capacity pricing (2026-08-06)

A ledger entry that states a want without pricing the capacity to pursue it is a
portrait, and portraits both-side. "Merz wants to lead" is a portrait; "Merz wants
to lead and cannot pay — coalition at 57%, expected collapse, September elections
pending" is a judgment, because it can be wrong.
