# DEED A2 — READ THE INVOLUNTARY

## MOTION

Begins at a reaction outside a party's control; ends in a typed
finding plus the move it selects.

## WALK

Fix the unwilled sign as sourced fact; state what the party would
have shown had the suspected condition been false — the involuntary
testifies because it cannot be chosen; render the proof as a
conditional, weigh it (proof, indication, consistency), and hand it to
the next move in the same breath.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

Ignoring nonverbal and procedural evidence;
psychologizing ("he seemed rattled") without extracting the
conditional.

## GROUND

Metternich's surprise proving the secrecy pact (VIII); the
vote-proposal betraying intended use (IV); manner compared across
audiences (XI).

## WORKED PROOF

"The high representative praised his own instrument for one day
and hedged it the next — 'what happens next matters more.' A man does
not hedge a machine he believes self-executing. The hedge is testimony:
the drafters know the paper is a promissory note. The move it selects:
press him, not the parties, for the verifier's names — he has already
confessed he needs them."
