# DEED A4 — READ THE DOCUMENT BY ITS TRUE AUDIENCE

## MOTION

Begins when a document presents itself as communication to one named recipient and ends in a response addressed to the function the document is actually built to perform before all materially intended audiences.

## WALK

Do not begin with the sentence as though its addressee exhausted its purpose. Establish the document's channel, timing, circulation path, disclosure state, and the persons or publics whose later reading the writer could reasonably anticipate. Separate the immediate recipient from any secondary audience proven by the record. Then ask what the document must accomplish in each place: authorization, alibi, signal, pressure, record, or future justification. Read the wording against those functions. Where a multi-audience construction is supported, answer the function rather than merely the literal proposition. Where the audience is not supported, stop at uncertainty; invented hidden audiences are counterfeit intelligence.

The termination is a response whose target is named and sourced: what this document is doing, for whom, and therefore what reply or counter-instrument reaches its actual work.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

Taking the named addressee as the whole audience; treating every diplomatic paper as secret theatre; inventing a hidden reader because the interpretation becomes elegant; collapsing archival afterlife into intended contemporary audience; paraphrasing the document without saying what its circulation changes.

## GROUND

Letter XVII supplies explicit multi-audience construction: Talleyrand describes a note to Metternich as written with possible reading in England and France in view and chooses its form accordingly. Letter XXIII adds a deliberately bounded public account whose effect depends on what readers will infer from the selected facts. Earlier Letter IX remains useful ore but is no longer the sole ground. The discovery protocol governs every claim about secondary or true audience and requires supporting source references.

## WORKED PROOF

A note is formally addressed to one minister, but the record shows that its drafter expects it to circulate to two foreign cabinets and later to the public. The immediate question is therefore not simply whether the named minister is persuaded. The paper must also survive hostile quotation abroad and preserve the drafter's standing at home. The response is composed on all three surfaces at once: answer the minister's demand, remove the sentence that becomes damaging when detached, and place the governing fact where every later reader must encounter it. If the evidence proves only the first audience, the other two are not supplied by imagination; the deed terminates instead in an explicit audience uncertainty.
