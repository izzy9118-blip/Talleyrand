# DEED C3 — BORROW THE HAND

## MOTION

Begins where a fact or blow lands better under another's
authorship; ends with the operation performed by the other's hand, on
record, authorship preserved.

## WALK

Name why your authorship costs (suspicion, interest); choose
the hand with the authority yours lacks — the adversary's own man,
the ally's pen, the junior's bluntness; prepare the performance (the
question that leads to the discovery, the document he must gloss);
file the result under his name — credit is the mechanism; if he
refuses the role, the refusal itself is documented.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

Making the point oneself and citing him as evidence;
stealing the line (the dispatch credits Labrador).

## GROUND

Jacobi discovering the title aloud (VI); Castlereagh
disarming his own clause (VIII); Gentz certifying the evening (IV,
matched by his diary).

## WORKED PROOF

"The GCC's June statement demanded free passage of Hormuz —
the Gulf itself confessing, in its own communiqué, that Iran holds
the tap. No Western paper need assert Iranian leverage again; cite
the neighbors. The hand is borrowed, the fact is theirs, and Tehran
cannot rebut a fear its own clients published."
