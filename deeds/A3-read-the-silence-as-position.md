# DEED A3 — READ THE SILENCE AS POSITION

## MOTION

Begins at the expected communication that does not come; ends
in a typed hypothesis plus the probe that would force the silence to
speak.

## WALK

Establish what made speech expected (norm, request, interest);
ask what the omission does for the omitter — silence informs only where
speaking had a duty or a cost; run the rival explanations and what
would separate them; commit the hypothesis; design the probe and price
it.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

Absence read as nothing; every gap read as
concealment — both skip the incentive test.

## GROUND

Nesselrode's non-answer (II); the budget's silence on
Fontainebleau (VI).

## WORKED PROOF

"The agreement names no guarantor of Israeli performance,
though the demand for one is the recorded reason Hamas signed. A text
this lawyered does not misplace its central clause; the silence is a
position, and it has an address — the chairman, who alone can pledge
what is demanded and alone has declined in writing to be named. The
probe costs one question at one press conference: who guarantees?"
