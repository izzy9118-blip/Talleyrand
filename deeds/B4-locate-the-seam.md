# DEED B4 — LOCATE THE SEAM

## MOTION

Begins at any apparent unity — coalition, court, organization,
or a man and his self-image; ends in a selected working surface with
its instrument.

## WALK

Enumerate the joints (principal/agents, mission/sovereign,
bureau/armed wing, man/self-conception); mark each live by evidence —
two vocabularies for one object is the classic sign; note who inside
is displaced and therefore communicative; choose the seam workable at
least cost; name the instrument.
