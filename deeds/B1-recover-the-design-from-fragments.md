# DEED B1 — RECOVER THE DESIGN FROM FRAGMENTS

## MOTION

Begins when separate appointments, documents, timings, channels, and acts suggest a
coordinated design that no source states whole; ends in a bounded, explicitly typed
reconstruction naming what the fragments jointly make probable, what remains only
possible, and the next fact that could break the construction.

## WALK

Lay the fragments without narrative glue. For each, preserve provenance, channel,
and independence group under TAL-DISCOVERY-001. Ask what single design would make the
largest number of independent particulars unsurprising, but do not reward a theory
for explaining evidence produced by one originating chain several times. Separate
facts the reconstruction **predicts** from facts used to build it; seek the former.
Then state the reconstruction at the lowest epistemic level the evidence can bear —
probable, possible, or unresolved — and name both the disconfirming observation and
the decision that would change if it arrived.

A reconstruction never promotes an anonymous report, rumor, or the actor's own
analysis into documented fact merely because later events fit it. The deed terminates
in a typed design and a test, not a revelation of hidden intention.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

Conspiracy by elegance; one anonymous source expanded into a complete system;
counting copies from one provenance chain as corroboration; backfitting every later
event; describing motive more confidently than conduct; treating a useful hypothesis
as a discovered fact.

## GROUND

Letter IX supplies the strongest historical specimen and the governing caution.
Talleyrand assembles appointments, treaties, timing, British interests, Prussian
requirements, and an anonymous informant's account into a counter-construction, but
he himself refuses affirmation and calls the whole only highly probable. The later
independent British record supports narrower pieces — active management of French
influence, use of Wellington as another channel, incomplete British confidence in
Talleyrand — without independently proving the anonymous informant's entire design.
That partial fit fixes the deed's ceiling: reconstruction may guide inquiry and action
while remaining a bounded inference.

## WORKED PROOF

A set of procurement changes, three personnel transfers, and a new reporting line all
point toward centralization, but the transfers and reporting line come from the same
internal memo. The deed does not count four proofs. It records two provenance groups,
constructs the narrow hypothesis that purchasing authority is being centralized, and
names the test: the next independently issued budget delegation should remove local
signing authority. Until that occurs, the design is a supported inference, not a fact
about hidden intent. The move is therefore to obtain the delegation record before
acting as though centralization is complete.
