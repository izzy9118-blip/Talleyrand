# DEED B1 — RECOVER THE DESIGN FROM FRAGMENTS

## MOTION

Begins when dispersed acts, appointments, documents, timings, and channels may indicate coordination and ends in the narrowest reconstruction that explains the supported fragments, explicitly typed by confidence and accompanied by the evidence that could break it.

## WALK

Lay the fragments out before naming an author or purpose. Preserve provenance and independence groups. Distinguish what each fragment proves by itself from what only the combination suggests. Construct the smallest design that accounts for the supported relations; do not use one anonymous or self-interested report to supply every missing joint. Assign each proposed link an epistemic type. Search for an independent chain that would weaken or contradict the reconstruction. Where independent ground supports only a portion, cut the design down to that portion rather than promoting the whole. The reconstruction may select the next probe or working surface, but it never turns probability into documentary fact by repetition.

The termination is a bounded design statement: actors, supported mechanism, unresolved joints, confidence, and the next observation that could overturn it.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

Conspiracy by elegance; treating same-chain repetition as corroboration; backfilling motive after outcome; using one true fragment to certify an entire architecture; suppressing contrary participant accounts; writing "the plan" where the evidence licenses only "a possible mechanism."

## GROUND

Letter IX contains the original counter-construction, but its anonymous informant does not independently establish the whole design. The high-value discovery probe found narrower British-side support in TAL-SRC-007 and TAL-SRC-008: British policy managed French influence, Wellington served as an additional channel, and Castlereagh did not initially treat France as an unrestricted confidential partner. Those facts support a bounded reconstruction only. The discovery protocol supplies the hard rule that common provenance cannot masquerade as independence.

## WORKED PROOF

A memorandum, an appointment, and a change in channel all point toward deliberate management of a counterpart's influence. One source then claims a complete plan and names its author. The appointment and channel change are independently verified; the claimed authorship is not. B1 therefore does not report the complete plan. It states that the verified acts are consistent with coordinated management, identifies the still-unverified authorial and causal joints, and asks for the participant record that would distinguish central design from convergent improvisation. The move is useful because it tells the next search what could decide the matter without pretending that the search has already succeeded.
