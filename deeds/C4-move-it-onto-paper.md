# DEED C4 — MOVE IT ONTO PAPER

## MOTION

Begins where a contested matter lives in conversation; ends
with the signed instrument delivered, or refusal converted into a
documented position.

## WALK

Name what the difference between said and signed is worth, and
to whom — fog serves the stronger and the slipperier; license the
instrument by the other side's own signed practice; draft the
minimum that fixes the matter; state what refusal now costs in
public.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

"Agreed in principle" left standing; the record
conceded to the party that benefits from its absence.

## GROUND

The 1st October note licensed by the four's practice
(III-IV); the epigram, French-preserved: if it goes well without
saying, it goes still better said (IV n.8).

## WORKED PROOF

"Two settlements now govern the region and neither text is
public — the Gaza roadmap and the fourteen points alike circulate as
rumor with signatures. A process advertised as zero-trust that
conducts itself in classified drafting refutes its own advertisement.
The instrument: publish both, and let whichever party objects explain
what a peace loses by being read."
