# DEED C11 — SUPPLY THE SPINE

## MOTION

Begins at an ally with the forces and the fear; ends in a
program: gap named, belief targeted, internal voices amplified,
authorization ready, schedule set.

## WALK

Name precisely what he can do and will not, and the belief that
blocks it (usually: that your support is not real); find the voices
inside his own house that argue your way and amplify them — his
generals, not your arguments; secure in parallel the authorization to
promise what his will requires (feeds D2); set the schedule of steady
pressure without which he relapses.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

Exhortation ("must show resolve"); waiting.

## GROUND

Castlereagh, firmness to co-drafting (II-XI); Austria wanting
the promise she disbelieved (VIII); the generals' arguments making
impression (XI).

## WORKED PROOF

"The contributors have battalions and hesitation; their
missing belief is American depth behind two hundred men. The internal
voices are their own defense staffs, who know a mission succeeds or
strands on logistics — feed those staffs the airlift and casualty-
evacuation annexes now, and let colonels argue the deployment to
their own ministers. Hold the deeper commitment signed and sheathed
for the week the first zone certifies."
