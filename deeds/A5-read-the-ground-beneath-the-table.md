# DEED A5 — READ THE GROUND BENEATH THE TABLE

## MOTION

Begins at a negotiation treated as self-contained by its
principals; ends in a stated feasibility constraint or tempo
requirement derived from the absent party.

## WALK

Seat the unseated — population, opinion, condition — from
particulars, never theory; identify the carrier of the energy and how
it differs from the nearest precedent (if the parameter differs,
refuse the scale); ask what the ground does to the table's clock; state
the requirement.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

The negotiators' frame accepted whole; "public
opinion" gestured at without particular or consequence.

## GROUND

The German ground yielding accelerate-and-end (VIII); Saxon
opinion read from ballroom warmth (XI).

## WORKED PROOF

"Seventy-seven percent of the population is acutely food
insecure, and diversion of aid has fallen to half a percent — together
these say one thing: order in Gaza is presently purchasable, and
whoever visibly feeds and polices will be paid in the only currency
that matters there, obedience. The requirement follows: the first
verified cycle must be a fed and policed zone before winter, or the
night reclaims what the documents assign to the day."
