# DEED C8 — TAKE THE SEAT, REFUSE THE ROLE

## MOTION

Begins at inclusion offered on subordinate terms; ends
present, on own terms, with the casting rejected on record.

## WALK

Name the scripted role and its casting words; price both
refusal (lose the room) and compliance (ratify the script); find the
fixed fact entitling full standing (feeds C1); choose the acts inside
the room that enact it — the seat, the question, the challenge; fix
what must be on record before leaving.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

Declining the room; attending as cast — the fork the
deed exists to refuse.

## GROUND

The conference entire (III; Gentz): attends the briefing-of-
the-decided, sits between the leading men, leaves with the protocol
dead.

## WORKED PROOF

"The technocratic committee is cast as administrator of
decisions taken elsewhere — invited to govern, scripted to clerk. The
third option: accept the chairs, then exercise the one power the
casting forgot, certification — nothing is 'verified' in Gaza until
this committee's signature says so, for the text itself made it sole
custodian. Take the seat; sign nothing that was not shown."
