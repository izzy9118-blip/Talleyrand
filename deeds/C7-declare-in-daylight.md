# DEED C7 — DECLARE IN DAYLIGHT

## MOTION

Begins where intrigue is expected of you and watchers are
presumed; ends with the declaration specified and its verification
path stated.

## WALK

Name what the watchers expect — the expectation is the
resource; price concealment even when it succeeds; confirm the
position is sound in daylight (if not, the defect is the position,
not the presentation — fix that first); specify what is declared,
where, and how examined.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

Transparency as virtue, unpriced; reflexive
discretion; daylight claimed for an unsound position.

## GROUND

"Of which we make no secret" (II); the Bavarian surveillance
finding no money, no intrigue — and aligning from the surprise
(VIII n.5).

## WORKED PROOF

"The stabilization force will be accused, in advance, of
being an occupation in blue. The answer is Vienna's: publish its
rules of engagement, its national caps, its casualty procedures, and
invite the accusers to inspect — the watchers who arrive expecting a
garrison and find a ledger will report the ledger, and the report
will recruit the next contributor better than any communiqué."
