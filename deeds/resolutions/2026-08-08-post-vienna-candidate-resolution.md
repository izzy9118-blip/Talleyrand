# POST-VIENNA CANDIDATE RESOLUTION

The purpose of this record is subtraction as much as addition. A candidate has no
authority merely because it is interesting. The extended Vienna reading and the
independent-source probe were required to determine what deserves deed form.

## DRAFTED TO THE KEEL

### A4 — READ THE DOCUMENT BY ITS TRUE AUDIENCE

**Disposition:** drafted as `deeds/A4-read-the-document-by-its-true-audience.md`.

Reason: deliberate multi-audience document construction recurs in later ground and
is distinct from merely reading designed wording. The discovery protocol supplies a
hard failure against imaginary hidden audiences.

### B1 — RECOVER THE DESIGN FROM FRAGMENTS

**Disposition:** drafted in bounded form as `deeds/B1-recover-the-design-from-fragments.md`.

Reason: the capacity is real, but its historical specimen also supplies the limit.
The Letter IX construction is explicitly probability-typed by Talleyrand and only
partially corroborated by independent British records. The deed therefore terminates
in a typed reconstruction and a disconfirmation test, never a declaration of hidden
intent as fact.

### B7 — REFUSE THE FALSE SCALE

**Disposition:** drafted as `deeds/B7-refuse-the-false-scale.md`.

Reason: the same motion recurs across social precedent, political aggregates,
population valuation, territory, and military geography. It is distinct from A5:
A5 seats omitted ground; B7 decides whether the offered scale can measure that ground.

## RETIRED AS SEPARATE DEEDS

### A6 — READ THE PERIPHERY AS GAUGE

**Disposition:** `ABSORBED_NOT_DRAFTED`.

The smaller or less independent actor can indeed register pressure early, but the
operation is not distinct enough to carry a deed. The material is already governed
by A5 (seat the ground beneath the table) and B4 (locate the seam). A separate A6
would multiply names without adding a new motion.

### B5 — MODEL THE SECOND ORDER

**Disposition:** `ABSORBED_NOT_DRAFTED`.

The useful part survives inside B1 plus TAL-DISCOVERY-001: ask what evidence a
second-order hypothesis predicts, type it, and seek independent ground. A free-standing
B5 would reward speculative cleverness and recreate the counterfeit of an elegant
hidden design without a falsifiable chain.

### C9 — FIGHT WHERE THE CAUSES CONVERGE

**Disposition:** `ABSORBED_NOT_DRAFTED`.

Its precondition was already extracted into Deed 0, SEE ONE BOARD, whose terminus is
the convergence square. The rest of C9 is executed by existing deeds once that square
is selected. Reintroducing C9 would duplicate the architecture the keel deliberately
separated.

### C10 — CONVERT THE ALLY'S FRUSTRATION

**Disposition:** `RETIRED_CURRENT_FORMULATION`.

The fact of later alignment survives; the one-way conversion story does not. The
independent Castlereagh account gives Wellington causal weight and describes
Talleyrand as becoming more accommodating. A future deed about reciprocal alignment
through multiple channels may be built if the corpus requires it, but no replacement
is presumed merely to save the candidate.

### D1 — BIND THE PRINCIPAL BY ATTRIBUTION

**Disposition:** `RETIRED_CURRENT_FORMULATION`.

The extended correspondence strengthens Louis XVIII as an independently reasoning
sovereign principal. The old formulation risks turning reciprocal authorization and
doctrinal alignment into psychological capture by the minister. It may not enter the
deed corpus.

## PRESERVED AS UNRESOLVED ORE

### C5 — BUILD THE DOOR FOR HIS WORD

**Disposition:** `CANDIDATE_HT_HOLD`.

The operation is coherent and thickened, but the decisive Alexander dialogue remains
self-testimonial after an independent-source probe. Preserve; do not draft merely
because no contradiction was found.

### C6 — PRE-LOAD THE CHANNEL, READ THE GREETING

**Disposition:** `CANDIDATE_HT_HOLD`.

Channel selection is securely incorporated into TAL-DISCOVERY-001. The distinctive
greeting/audience episode remains self-testimonial. Preserve as ore until independent
ground appears or later corpus renders the separate deed unnecessary.

## HELD-OUT REGISTER ITEMS

H1-H4 remain outside the deed corpus under their existing reasons. This resolution
does not promote them.

# RESULT

The proposed deed corpus gains three deeds and deliberately refuses seven separate
promotions. The operating corpus is therefore narrower after more reading, not larger
by default. No deed becomes owner-ratified through this record.
