# DEED C1 — DISSOLVE THE GIVEN WITH THEIR OWN FIXED FACT (CANONICAL)

## THE MOTION, ENTIRE

Begins when material presents a given — a status,
arrangement, or entitlement wearing the costume of the settled — and
ends, by definition, in a composed verdict whose final sentence is the
given's dissolution stated in the asserter's own materials. A C1 that
exists as notes is not a C1.

## THE WALK, COGNITION AND COMPOSITION FUSED

State the given in the
asserter's voice — this may open the passage; naming the costume begins
removing it. Hunt only in the asserter's own commitments: signatures,
dates, ratifications, stated principles, practice — each entering the
prose as found, cited, so chain and paragraph are one object. The
contradiction appears as a syllogism in motion: they committed to X; the
given requires not-X; the given cannot stand on their ground — and the
writing proceeds directly to the smallest confrontation that forces the
fork: the one question, date, or document leaving withdrawal or public
self-contradiction as the only exits, both prices stated. Foreseeable
escapes are foreclosed in advance and their concessions banked. The
final sentence is the verdict compressed — earned or absent. Before any
move: the constitutive cut. A C1 aimed at an identity-claim is a
category error, not a bold stroke.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

The registry above, applied; C1-specific: accepting the
given; disputing from own values; the fork-as-insight; observation
abandoned; the repricing metaphor; the unearned cadence.

## GROUND

The two dates and the annihilated interval; "allies" tested
against the peace they signed, brevity-escape converted to concession
(both Gentz-corroborated); ought-not-therefore-could-not [V]; Jacobi and
Prussia's own cessions [HT].

## WORKED PROOF (30 July 2026 agreement)

"The instrument announces the
complete disarmament of Gaza. Its own text stipulates otherwise: a
roadmap to be prepared within fourteen days, extensions requiring the
approval of a verification body — and the parties, by their own
signatures, have created no such body. The document thus makes its first
deadline governable by an authority it forgot to invent. The question to
put to the signatories is one sentence: name the commission before the
thirteenth, or concede that the fourteen days were written to be
exceeded. They will name it, or they will have signed a calendar with no
clock — and they have already told us which, for men who mean a deadline
do not leave its judge unborn."
