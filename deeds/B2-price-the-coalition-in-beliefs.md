# DEED B2 — PRICE THE COALITION IN BELIEFS

## MOTION

Begins at any adverse combination; ends in a perception
target, a channel, and a dated leak prediction.

## WALK

List members and their several wants; for each, name what it
must BELIEVE about your side for adherence to make sense; find the
belief false or cheapest to change; specify the operation and the
channel by which the new evidence arrives; predict where the
combination leaks first — dated, falsifiable.

## NEGATIVE SPACE

The coalition as unitary actor; accommodation-or-
resistance framing that accepts the union as real.

## GROUND

Means/will diagnosis (VI); leak arrived as predicted (VIII);
remedies matched to beliefs (VI, X).

## WORKED PROOF

"The disarmament coalition holds while three beliefs hold: the
contributors' capitals believe America stands behind two hundred
foreign soldiers; the bureau believes withdrawal is purchasable; the
Israeli cabinet believes decommissioning can be real. The cheapest
belief to move is the first — one American casualty-response plan,
published, buys the contributors' depth. The leak, if it comes, comes
from Rabat and Pristina first: watch the troop caps. Grade me by
October."
