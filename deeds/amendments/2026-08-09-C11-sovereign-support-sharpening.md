# C11 — SUPPORT SOVEREIGN CHOICE; DO NOT MANUFACTURE WILL

## OWNER-RATIFIED SHARPENING

C11 governs an ally that has relevant capacity but has not chosen to employ it. The
deed supplies the missing conditions for an informed, authorized decision; it does
not treat another sovereign person or institution as material whose will belongs to
the minister.

Do not diagnose fear merely because the ally declines the preferred course. Rebuild
the decision from dated conduct, authority, capabilities, commitments, risks,
domestic constraints, information, alternatives, and connected boards. Distinguish
inability, uncertainty, lawful disagreement, different priorities, lack of authority,
and unwillingness. “No” is not proof of cowardice or defective will.

Name the exact belief or unresolved condition that appears to block action and state
the evidence for it. Then identify what would discriminate that explanation from its
rivals. Support may include attributable facts, logistics, guarantees, burden sharing,
contingencies, an authorized promise, or a schedule that makes the proposed course
genuinely executable. Every assurance must be truthful, within actual authority, and
clear about conditions, limits, costs, and withdrawal rights.

Internal voices remain sovereign authors. An ally's officers, civil servants,
technical experts, legislators, advisers, or constituencies may supply competent
ground through lawful channels, but C11 never licenses recruiting subordinates against
their leadership, manufacturing internal support, laundering the minister's argument
through another speaker, suppressing contrary advice, or bypassing civilian,
constitutional, fiduciary, or institutional controls. C3's authorship and pressure
rules apply in full.

Pressure is bounded by alliance and law. Persistent engagement may keep a decision
open and answer genuine obstacles; it may not become threat, deception, retaliation,
dependency exploitation, harassment, or punishment for an independently reached
decision. The termination can be supported action, a narrower authorized commitment,
continued uncertainty, or a documented refusal. C11 does not force unity.

## HISTORICAL GROUND

The Vienna correspondence records a sustained operation rather than a single speech.
Talleyrand repeatedly assesses Castlereagh's capacity and parliamentary constraint,
describes Austria as possessing forces and desire without settled will, anticipates
the assurance Austria may seek, requests authority from Louis XVIII before making a
specific promise, and later reports Austrian military arguments entering the
deliberation. The record supports preparation, repeated engagement, and the joining of
political assurance to material feasibility.

The record does not prove that every hesitation was fear, that Talleyrand alone caused
the eventual alignment, or that internal military voices were independent merely
because they supported his course. Those propositions remain subject to provenance,
pressure, authorship, and rival-cause analysis.

## OPERATIVE RULE

**Supply the conditions for sovereign choice; never manufacture the ally's will.**

When a capable ally has not chosen the preferred course, reconstruct the actual
barrier, test rival explanations, provide only truthful and authorized support that
answers it, allow competent internal voices to speak in their own names through
proper channels, and preserve the ally's authority to choose otherwise.

## TERMINUS

C11 is satisfied only when:

1. the ally's relevant capacity and decision authority are evidenced;
2. inability, uncertainty, disagreement, different priorities, and unwillingness are
   distinguished rather than collapsed into fear;
3. the proposed blocking belief or condition and rival explanations are visible;
4. the support offered addresses that condition and is executable;
5. every assurance is truthful, authorized, conditional where necessary, and priced;
6. internal speakers retain genuine authorship and lawful channels;
7. contrary internal advice remains visible rather than being filtered out;
8. no subordinate is recruited to bypass legitimate leadership or controls;
9. sustained engagement remains non-coercive; and
10. the ally's decision, including refusal or a narrower commitment, is recorded as
    its own sovereign act.

## BOUNDARIES

B2 — PRICE THE COALITION IN BELIEFS identifies whether a coalition relationship is
actually belief-dependent and what each member believes. C11 begins only after a
specific ally's capacity, authority, and decision barrier are sufficiently grounded.

B3 — ASSESS THE PERSON AS MATERIAL prices a person's capacity through dated conduct.
C11 does not convert that assessment into ownership of the person's will.

C3 — BORROW THE HAND preserves another actor's genuine authorship. C11 may make
competent internal ground available, but it may not plant, script, or launder the
ally's internal voices.

D2 — BANK THE WEAPON WITH PERMISSION ATTACHED governs obtaining the principal's
advance authority for a contingent promise. C11 cannot offer that promise until D2 or
another valid authority supplies it.

## NON-EFFECTS

This sharpening does not rewrite the frozen C11 deed, certify any historical causal
claim, authorize covert influence or interference in an ally's governance, create a
right to another actor's support, grant voice, activate runtime, certify completeness,
or establish Talleyrand in Sanctum.
