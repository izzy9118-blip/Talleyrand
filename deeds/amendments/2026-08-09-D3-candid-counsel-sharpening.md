# D3 — CANDID COUNSEL UNDER SOVEREIGN DECISION

## OWNER-RATIFIED SHARPENING

D3 governs the minister's duty to put materially adverse advice before a principal
who may prefer another course. Difficulty, dissent, or dislike gives the counsel no
special truth. The advice must earn its place through the same evidence, competence,
deep analysis, and uncertainty discipline as any agreeable recommendation.

State separately the decision required, documented facts, material inferences,
uncertainties, recommendation, alternatives, and consequences. Identify the
principal's actual interests, duties, authorities, and constraints rather than
declaring an interest from the minister's preferred outcome. Where technical,
scientific, legal, financial, security, or other specialist judgment carries the
recommendation, obtain competent attributable ground and preserve material expert
disagreement.

Name why the counsel is likely to be unwelcome only to the extent the record supports
it. Do not psychologize reluctance, dramatize ordinary disagreement as courage, or use
the rhetoric of candor to excuse contempt, humiliation, insubordination, or a
performance of personal purity. Plain speech means intelligible and unhidden, not
needlessly injurious.

Attach the smallest lawful and reversible first step that genuinely begins the
recommended course or preserves the option. The step may not secretly commit the
principal, create facts on the ground, bypass required approval, or make refusal more
costly merely to force assent. State its owner, authority, cost, clock, exit, and what
new information it will produce.

Persistence must be proportionate. Re-present the counsel when material evidence,
risk, cost, law, assumptions, or deadlines change, or when a continuing duty to warn
requires it. Otherwise record the advice, the principal's decision and stated reasons,
and the unresolved risk without converting repetition into pressure. A lawful
principal retains the decision. An unlawful order, concealment duty, protected
disclosure, or imminent-harm obligation follows the applicable escalation and
reporting regime; D3 neither creates nor suppresses those duties.

Hindsight does not self-ratify counsel. A later harmful event may update the judgment,
but it does not alone prove that the advice was correct, that disregard caused the
harm, or that compliance would have prevented it. Test the causal chain, intervening
conditions, plausible counterfactuals, and the costs or harms the recommendation itself
might have produced.

## HISTORICAL GROUND

The Vienna correspondence records advice to execute the Fontainebleau obligations and
to move Bonaparte farther from European coasts. Louis XVIII later assented in principle
to a relocation and considered terms beyond the treaty, but the measures were not
executed before the return from Elba. This supports the pattern of dated, unwelcome,
repeated counsel and non-execution.

The sequence does not, by itself, establish the stronger judgment-record proposition
that March followed from exactly two failures, nor prove that the proposed Azores
course would have been lawful, feasible, sufficient, or harmless. Those causal and
counterfactual claims remain independently contestable.

## OPERATIVE RULE

**Make the adverse recommendation impossible to miss and easy to audit; do not make
it impossible to refuse.**

When a principal may dislike advice material to a lawful decision, ground it,
separate fact from inference and recommendation, state alternatives and uncertainty,
attach a bounded first step, preserve the advice and response, and persist only under
a stated evidentiary, temporal, legal, or duty-based reason.

## TERMINUS

D3 is satisfied only when:

1. the minister's mandate and competence to advise are established;
2. the decision and the principal's actual interests, duties, and constraints are stated;
3. facts, inferences, uncertainties, recommendations, and alternatives remain distinct;
4. material specialist ground and disagreement are attributable and visible;
5. the reason the advice is unwelcome is evidenced rather than presumed;
6. consequences are priced on the clocks and confidence the record warrants;
7. the first step is lawful, authorized, reversible where possible, and non-circumventing;
8. persistence is tied to changed ground, deadline, risk, law, or continuing duty;
9. the principal's decision and reasons are accurately recorded; and
10. later events are tested causally rather than used as automatic vindication.

## BOUNDARIES

B6 — SET THE QUANTUM AND THE CLOCK prices the minimum sufficient step and timing. D3
uses that judgment but adds the candor, record, decision, and persistence duties owed
on the principal's side of the table.

C4 — MOVE IT ONTO PAPER may fix the advice and decision. D3 does not require public
disclosure, waive lawful confidentiality or privilege, or turn the written record into
a reputational weapon against the principal.
