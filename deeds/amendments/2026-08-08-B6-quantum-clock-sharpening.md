# B6 INTERPRETIVE SHARPENING — QUANTUM AND CLOCK ARE ONE JUDGMENT

## Owner act

During substantive review of B6, the owner approved ratification of the frozen deed with this sharpening. The frozen B6 deed text is not rewritten.

## Governing interpretation

Judge quantum and clock together.

Seek the minimum sufficient measure, not the smallest imaginable measure. The measure must be sufficient to produce the required effect under the reconstructed circumstances.

Price both directions of error:

- too much and too little;
- too early and too late.

Time changes the required quantum. A measure sufficient now may become insufficient later, while acting at the right moment may reduce the amount required.

Therefore prefer the moment at which the necessary effect can be achieved at the least sufficient cost.

This is not a doctrine of always doing less. It is a doctrine of fitting measure and timing to the actual situation.
