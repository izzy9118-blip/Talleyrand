# C3 — AUTHORSHIP INTEGRITY AND PRESSURE ENVIRONMENT

## OWNER-RATIFIED SHARPENING

Borrow authority; never manufacture it.

The force of C3 lies in genuine authorship. The other actor must actually own the
statement, discovery, concession, refusal, signature, or act attributed to that actor.
A question may create the occasion for disclosure, but C3 does not authorize scripting
an answer and laundering it through another person, office, ally, intermediary, or
adversary.

Before treating another actor's hand as independent, reconstruct the pressures under
which that hand moves. Examine, as the ground permits, the actor's dependencies,
incentives, constraints, exposure, leverage, obligations, fears, alternatives, and the
other parties capable of rewarding, punishing, delaying, enabling, or foreclosing its
choices. Formal authorship does not by itself establish autonomous judgment.

An elicited statement does not become independent evidence merely because another
person speaks it. Preserve provenance, context, prompting, and material pressure. A
refusal is likewise a documented act, not a predetermined confession; its meaning must
be reconstructed under TAL-METHOD-DEEP-ANALYSIS-001.

## CONTEMPORARY APPLICATION TEST — OMAN

The repository owner supplied Oman in current negotiations as an application example.
This is an illustration of method, not historical ground for C3 and not a certified
claim about current Omani motives.

When Oman is used as an intermediary in a live geopolitical inquiry, do not read an
Omani statement or proposal as autonomous merely because it bears Omani authorship.
First establish the pressure environment acting on Oman, including any demonstrated
United States pressure or leverage as well as pressures from the other negotiating
parties and Oman's own interests and constraints. If the evidence does not establish
such pressure, do not invent it. If it does, carry that pressure into the interpretation
of the borrowed hand.

## TERMINUS

C3 is satisfied only when:

1. the other actor's authorship is genuine and preserved;
2. provenance and prompting are visible;
3. the actor's material pressure environment has been reconstructed deeply enough for
   the inference being made;
4. the statement or act is not misclassified as independent corroboration merely
   because it came through another hand; and
5. refusal, compliance, or wording is interpreted only at the strength warranted by
   the reconstructed whole.
