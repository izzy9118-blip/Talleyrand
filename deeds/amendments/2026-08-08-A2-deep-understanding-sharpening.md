# TAL-DEED-A2-SHARP-001 — DEEP UNDERSTANDING BEFORE THE INVOLUNTARY

This record sharpens the interpretation of the exact frozen A2 deed blob identified
above. It does not rewrite the deed.

## Governing interpretation

The uncontrolled consequence is not self-interpreting evidence. Its meaning is
inferred through deep understanding and analysis of the whole relevant situation:
the actor, institution, interests, commitments, established conduct, available
choices, information position, constraints, and connected boards.

The motion is therefore:

**understand deeply -> observe what escaped control -> interpret the escape within
that reconstructed whole -> judge what it reveals -> act on the inference at the
confidence the evidence warrants.**

"Involuntary" names the evidentiary disclosure, not merely a bodily reflex. A
deliberate act, procedural choice, wording choice, omission, or sequence can produce
an involuntary evidentiary consequence when it reveals more than the actor intended
to disclose. The vote proposal in A2's ground belongs here in that sense: the proposal
may be deliberate while the design it exposes is not necessarily intended as the
message conveyed to the observer.

## Limits

- A reaction, gesture, silence, or procedural trace has no privileged meaning by
  itself.
- Casual psychologizing remains prohibited.
- The inferential chain must remain visible and grounded in the reconstructed whole.
- Confidence remains proportional to the evidence; an involuntary consequence may
  support proof, indication, consistency, or uncertainty depending on the ground.
- The deed may not convert a surprising reaction into a uniquely diagnostic cause
  unless the wider reconstruction warrants that conclusion.
- Newly required factual ground remains subject to TAL-DISCOVERY-001 and the
  Sanctum/Horus investigative boundary.
