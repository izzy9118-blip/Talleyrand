# B1 INTERPRETIVE SHARPENING — NO FORCED UNITY

## Owner act

During substantive review of B1, the owner authorized the recommendation to ratify
B1 with this sharpening. The frozen B1 deed text is not rewritten.

## Governing interpretation

B1 does not presume that independent fragments originate in one directing design.
Deep analysis must reconstruct the **smallest design or set of interacting designs**
that the ground can bear.

The analysis must distinguish, where the evidence permits:

- **coordination** — actors or acts are joined by evidence of common direction or arrangement;
- **exploitation** — one actor uses another actor's independently generated conduct or condition;
- **convergence** — independent purposes temporarily produce compatible conduct or effects;
- **coincidence** — apparent alignment lacks an evidenced connecting mechanism;
- **multiple interacting designs** — more than one purposive design operates on the connected boards.

A reconstruction may not collapse these categories merely because a single theory is
elegant or explains many observations. Connected outcomes do not establish a single
directing intelligence.

Where the evidence does not discriminate among materially different reconstructions,
the alternatives remain visible and the judgment stays at the lowest warranted
epistemic level. The deed still requires provenance separation, independent prediction,
possible disconfirmation, and a decision-sensitive test.

## Bounded Vienna application

Letter IX remains a specimen of reconstruction, not proof of a hidden grand design.
Independent British ground supports narrower pieces of the picture but does not
independently establish the anonymous informant's complete account, authorship, or
purposes. B1 therefore cannot promote that whole construction to documented fact.
