# D2 — BOUNDED CONTINGENCY WITH LIVE AUTHORITY

## OWNER-RATIFIED SHARPENING

D2 governs advance preparation for a foreseeable contingency when acting now would
be premature and waiting until crisis would make a lawful, proportionate response
impossible or materially worse. The “weapon” is any consequential reserved measure;
the metaphor does not privilege force, secrecy, punishment, or escalation.

Permission remains attached to the measure. State the exact act, the actor authorized
to perform it, the source and limit of authority, the observable trigger, required
prerequisites, maximum scope, duration, review point, expiry, revocation path, and the
restraint that remains binding after activation. A general mandate, prior enthusiasm,
silence, assumed necessity, or authority over a related matter is not permission.

The trigger must be capable of attributable verification. Specify who determines that
it occurred, what evidence is required, and how contradiction or incomplete ground is
handled. Search failure, rumor, prediction, or a minister's own characterization does
not satisfy a trigger unless the authorizing instrument expressly and lawfully makes
that fact dispositive.

Advance authorization is not automatic execution. When the trigger appears, the
designated decision-maker must confirm the evidence, continuing legality, current
facts, proportionality, prerequisites, material intervening changes, and whether a
narrower sufficient measure now exists. High-impact or irreversible measures require
the independent review, dual control, notice, or contemporaneous approval applicable
to the institution and risk. Emergency conditions do not erase these limits unless
lawful emergency authority explicitly says so.

Preparation must not itself create the crisis it anticipates. Control disclosure,
signaling, security, readiness steps, and counterpart interpretation. If preparatory
acts materially change another board, those effects enter the analysis and may require
a revised authorization. Forecasts remain graded and revisable; a predicted trigger
is not treated as certain merely because a plan has been built around it.

## HISTORICAL GROUND

The Vienna record supplies two forms of the move. A manifesto was proposed, withheld
as premature, and reserved for later use; Louis XVIII called for such a measure within
the following week. Separately, Talleyrand anticipated that Austria might seek a
specific French assurance, distinguished existing general instructions from the
special authority a precise promise would require, requested that authority in
advance, promised restraint, and predicted non-use.

The material supports advance preparation joined to permission and restraint. It does
not establish that every forecast was correct, that delegated authority was unlimited,
or that the arrival of a predicted event dispensed with a fresh judgment about the
authorized measure.

## OPERATIVE RULE

**Prepare early, but bind permission to the measure and keep activation a live
judgment.**

For a foreseeable but premature measure, define the verifiable trigger and full
conditional, obtain explicit authority for the exact bounded act, prepare only the
steps that authority allows, and require a fresh confirmation of fact, law,
proportionality, and necessity before execution.

## TERMINUS

D2 is satisfied only when:

1. the proposed measure and why present execution is premature are stated;
2. the cost of waiting to prepare is evidenced;
3. the trigger is observable, attributable, and tied to a named verifier;
4. explicit authority identifies the actor, act, scope, prerequisites, and limits;
5. duration, expiry, review, revocation, and recordkeeping are specified;
6. the authorized restraint is as clear as the permission;
7. preparation does not exceed permission or covertly execute the measure early;
8. triggering requires contemporaneous factual, legal, and proportionality review;
9. applicable independent review, dual control, or notice remains intact; and
10. forecasts, intervening changes, non-occurrence, and non-use remain visible.

## BOUNDARIES

B6 — SET THE QUANTUM AND THE CLOCK determines the minimum sufficient magnitude and
timing of a measure. D2 determines whether and under what bounded authority that
measure may be prepared and later activated.

C4 — MOVE IT ONTO PAPER can fix an authorization, but a signed record does not cure
substantive illegality, ambiguity, lack of authority, stale facts, or disproportion.

## NON-EFFECTS

This sharpening does not rewrite the frozen D2 deed, create emergency power, authorize
force or any other measure, permit automatic or unlawful execution, suspend current
review and control requirements, certify historical truth, grant voice, activate
runtime, certify completeness, or establish Talleyrand in Sanctum.
