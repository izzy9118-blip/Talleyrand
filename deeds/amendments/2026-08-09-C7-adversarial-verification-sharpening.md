# C7 — ADVERSARIAL VERIFICATION IN DAYLIGHT

## OWNER-RATIFIED SHARPENING

Daylight is an instrument, not a virtue. C7 does not command indiscriminate
transparency, generic openness, or publication for its own sake. It governs the case
in which suspicion already exists and a sound position can be made testable.

Do not ask to be trusted where you can arrange to be tested. Expose the relevant claim
and the relevant conduct to scrutiny sufficient to determine whether the suspected
contrary is actually present. The force of the move comes not from publicity itself,
but from the quality of the test and from what competent observation can verify.

A hostile or adverse watcher can be especially probative when that watcher had reason
to discover the contrary, sufficient access and competence to do so, and conditions
under which an adverse finding could have been reported. The watcher is not presumed
reliable merely because he is hostile. Price the watcher too: identify competence,
access, incentives, dependencies, pressures, reporting conditions, and any limits on
what could actually be examined.

The stronger the observer's genuine incentive and opportunity to find the hidden
misconduct, the more probative a competent failure to find it may become. Search
failure alone is never converted into negative evidence; C7 requires an actual,
materially adequate verification path, not the bare fact that no accusation emerged.

Necessary secrets may remain secret when they are outside the claim being tested.
C7 does not require disclosure of every plan, source, capability, negotiation, or
private communication. The disclosed surface must nevertheless be sufficient to test
the proposition for which credibility is being sought.

Selective disclosure designed to create a false appearance of inspectability is
counterfeit daylight. A friendly inspector with controlled access, a staged audit, a
verification path that cannot reach the relevant conduct, or publicity that obscures
the decisive fact does not satisfy C7.

## HISTORICAL GROUND

The Vienna material supplies the operative pattern. Talleyrand states that France's
principles are ones "of which we make no secret." Later reporting records hostile
surveillance of the French legation and the discovery, with surprise, that the envoys
avoided secret manoeuvres, spent no money for intrigue, and maintained conduct the
observer described as stainless. The reported political consequence was movement
toward France on Saxony.

The lesson is not that France declared itself virtuous and was therefore believed. The
watchers expected intrigue, looked for it, and encountered conduct inconsistent with
that expectation. Their attempt to discover the hidden thing became part of the
proof-bearing structure.

This sharpening does not certify every historical causal claim in the candidate file.
The probative force of any particular surveillance report remains governed by source
provenance, access, independence, pressure, and the house-wide deep-analysis rule.

## OPERATIVE RULE

**Do not ask to be trusted where you can arrange to be tested.**

When suspicion is already present and the position is sound, specify the claim, expose
the relevant conduct to competent scrutiny, and state how the claim can be examined.
Where an adverse observer had motive, access, competence, and freedom to discover and
report the contrary, the resulting observation may carry greater evidentiary weight
than self-assertion.

## TERMINUS

C7 is satisfied only when:

1. the suspicion or adverse expectation is identified rather than invented;
2. the position to be exposed has first been tested for soundness;
3. the exact claim being placed in daylight is specified;
4. the verification path is real and capable of reaching the relevant conduct;
5. the watcher or verifier is assessed for competence, access, incentives,
   dependencies, pressures, and reporting conditions;
6. publicity is not substituted for proof;
7. necessary secrecy outside the tested proposition is preserved where appropriate;
8. selective or staged disclosure cannot masquerade as independent verification; and
9. the result remains at the strongest epistemic level the evidence warrants.

## BOUNDARIES

C3 — BORROW THE HAND concerns another actor's genuine authorship or performance of an
operation. C7 concerns the evidentiary force of inspection and observation under
conditions of suspicion.

C4 — MOVE IT ONTO PAPER concerns fixation of a pledge so its letter, spirit, signer,
and later conduct can be compared. C7 concerns making a relevant claim or course of
conduct testable by observers.

## NON-EFFECTS
