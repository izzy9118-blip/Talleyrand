# B4 INTERPRETIVE SHARPENING — THE SEAM MUST BE EVIDENCED

## Owner act

During substantive review of B4, the owner authorized ratification of the frozen
B4 deed with this sharpening. The frozen B4 deed text is not rewritten.

## Governing interpretation

A seam is an evidenced divergence that changes what can actually be done. The
analysis must not presume that an apparent unity contains a usable seam merely
because its members disagree, use different vocabularies, occupy different
positions, resent one another, or include a displaced person.

Reconstruct the apparent unity first. Then test, as the ground permits, divergences
in:

- authority and formal competence;
- interests and desired outcomes;
- commitments and obligations;
- information and access;
- capacity and control;
- incentives, fears, dependencies, and alternatives;
- channels to the operative decision node;
- self-conception where documented conduct makes it analytically relevant.

Vocabulary, resentment, displacement, disagreement, or differing institutional
positions may indicate where to look. None of them proves a seam by itself.

## Causal requirement

Before B4 may select a working surface, the analysis must state the transmission
mechanism by which the identified divergence can alter the object of action.

The chain is:

1. identify the divergence;
2. establish it from attributable ground;
3. state the causal or institutional mechanism through which it matters;
4. identify who can act through the seam;
5. show how the seam reaches the real decision node or operative constraint;
6. select the least-cost instrument proportionate to the evidence;
7. specify an observable result that would support or disconfirm the seam judgment.

A weakness that cannot reach the object to be moved is not yet a working seam.

## No manufactured seam

If deep reconstruction shows the apparent unity remains operationally cohesive for
the matter at hand, the correct B4 result is `NO_WORKABLE_SEAM`. The deed must not
manufacture a fracture to satisfy its own method.

The existence of different vocabularies or internal disagreement is compatible
with real cohesion. Conversely, formal unity is compatible with a real operational
seam when the evidence establishes divergent control, incentives, capacities,
commitments, or channels that can affect the decision.

## Displaced persons and sources

A displaced or aggrieved person may possess information or access, but displacement
does not make that person truthful, reliable, reachable, or willing to communicate.
Source claims remain governed by TAL-DISCOVERY-001, including provenance,
corroboration, source-chain separation, and confidence limits.

## Connected boards

A seam may connect distinct boards, but a cross-board seam exists only where an
evidenced transmission mechanism connects them. Proximity, simultaneity, shared
personnel, or thematic resemblance alone is insufficient.

## Boundary

This sharpening does not grant voice, activate runtime, certify completeness or
historical truth, alter TAL-DISCOVERY-001, or establish Talleyrand in Sanctum.
