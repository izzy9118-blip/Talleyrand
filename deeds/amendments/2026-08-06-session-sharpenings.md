# Deed Sharpenings — 6 August 2026

Two sharpenings drawn out of the 5–6 August runs. Both are amendments to
canonical-draft material and are themselves canonical draft: they are proposed,
not ratified, and ratification per deed remains the owner's.

Neither is a new deed. Each tightens an existing motion at the point where the
session watched it fail.

## 1. B3 — the capacity-pricing requirement

**Amends:** [`../B3-assess-the-person-as-material.md`](../B3-assess-the-person-as-material.md)

B3 already gates the ledger entry on a conduct file of dated instances. The
session found the gate insufficient: a conduct file can be present and the entry
still be a portrait, because the entry states what the man *wants* without pricing
what he *can pay*.

**Requirement added.** A person-ledger entry that states a want without pricing
the capacity to pursue it is a portrait. Portraits both-side: they can be written
about anyone, they commit to nothing, and they cannot be graded.

Worked contrast, from the session:

- *Portrait:* "Merz wants to lead."
- *Judgment:* "Merz wants to lead and cannot pay — coalition at 57%, expected
  collapse, September elections pending."

The second is a judgment because it can be wrong. The want is priced against the
capacity, the capacity is dated and numbered, and a reader can hold the entry
against the record in September.

**Where it binds in the walk.** After the conduct file and before the assignment:
what he controls and what controls him is not answered until the price of his
stated aim is on the page beside it.

**Counterfeit it forecloses:** the balanced pen-portrait assigning nothing, which
B3 already names, in the specific form the session produced — the portrait that
survives the conduct-file gate by wearing dated instances while still pricing
nothing.

## 2. Discovery protocol — precondition 1, sharpened

**Status of the host record:** the discovery protocol is not yet a committed
record in this house. It exists in the session's working practice, and this
amendment is written so that its first precondition exists in the estate before
the protocol around it does. The protocol itself remains to be drafted and
ratified.

**Precondition 1, as sharpened, has two parts.**

### (a) Parity includes language-tier parity

Parity of file depth is not achieved while a principal is heard only in
translation. The Horus charter now states this as law (HORUS-AMD-001) and the
gate enforces it: T1 is filled only on original-language primary matter, and the
parity manifest records per principal whether he was heard in his own words.

The consequence for a deed: **no judgment about a principal may rise above the
tier at which that principal was actually heard.** A board can be thick in
aggregate and empty at exactly the principal a stroke depends on.

### (b) The board speaks before the minister reads

The primary sources are laid out, in their own words, before any deed fires.

The reason is the invisible curator, observed from the inside during this
session: when gathering and reading run in one voice, the gathering bends toward
the reading being formed. The frame sets early, and everything gathered after it
is gathered to serve it. Laying the board out first is what prevents the frame
from setting before the ground is heard.

This is why the board is closed before it is read. A closed board cannot be
topped up with the one fact a forming reading turns out to need — and that fact,
arriving late and fitting perfectly, is the strongest evidence that the curator
was at work.

**Relation to the keel.** Principle 7 already holds the briefing apart as an
artifact, facts entering the report only as levers at the moment of use. This
sharpening adds the temporal half: the artifact is complete and frozen before the
first lever is picked up.
