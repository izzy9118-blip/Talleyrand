# Historical Worked Examples Removed From Live Deeds

These examples are preserved exactly as historical pre-ratification applications.
They are **not** governing deed text and must not be loaded as evidence or prior
conclusions in a later Assembly inquiry.

## Deed 0 — SEE ONE BOARD

> "Gaza is not the file; it is the square presented. The board:
> a roadmap due the 13th, an Iranian expiry the 20th, a Lebanese track
> signed but bleeding, a Syrian chair newly at the American table, a
> Gulf price-list published in June, and an autumn election that is the
> true calendar of every man who can read. The convergence square is the
> verifier: nuclear inspection, Gaza decommissioning, and Lebanese
> disarmament are one profession — whoever builds the credible
> verification body for one holds the template for all three, and the
> smallest square on the board turns out to command it."

## A1 — READ THE DESIGNED WORD

> "The text promises withdrawal 'as' disarmament completes. 'As'
> was chosen over 'after' and over 'upon a schedule of' because it alone
> was signable by both — it promises simultaneity and specifies no
> increment, no first mover, no remedy for stall. The reply is not to
> protest the word but to replace it with arithmetic: propose the first
> increment pair — one zone certified, one withdrawal step dated — and
> 'as' acquires the calendar its drafters withheld."

## A2 — READ THE INVOLUNTARY

> "The high representative praised his own instrument for one day
> and hedged it the next — 'what happens next matters more.' A man does
> not hedge a machine he believes self-executing. The hedge is testimony:
> the drafters know the paper is a promissory note. The move it selects:
> press him, not the parties, for the verifier's names — he has already
> confessed he needs them."

## A3 — READ THE SILENCE AS POSITION

> "The agreement names no guarantor of Israeli performance,
> though the demand for one is the recorded reason Hamas signed. A text
> this lawyered does not misplace its central clause; the silence is a
> position, and it has an address — the chairman, who alone can pledge
> what is demanded and alone has declined in writing to be named. The
> probe costs one question at one press conference: who guarantees?"

## A5 — READ THE GROUND BENEATH THE TABLE

> "Seventy-seven percent of the population is acutely food
> insecure, and diversion of aid has fallen to half a percent — together
> these say one thing: order in Gaza is presently purchasable, and
> whoever visibly feeds and polices will be paid in the only currency
> that matters there, obedience. The requirement follows: the first
> verified cycle must be a fed and policed zone before winter, or the
> night reclaims what the documents assign to the day."

## B2 — PRICE THE COALITION IN BELIEFS

> "The disarmament coalition holds while three beliefs hold: the
> contributors' capitals believe America stands behind two hundred
> foreign soldiers; the bureau believes withdrawal is purchasable; the
> Israeli cabinet believes decommissioning can be real. The cheapest
> belief to move is the first — one American casualty-response plan,
> published, buys the contributors' depth. The leak, if it comes, comes
> from Rabat and Pristina first: watch the troop caps. Grade me by
> October."

## B3 — ASSESS THE PERSON AS MATERIAL

> "Entry: the chairman of the Board. Conduct file: chairs the
> body himself rather than appointing (Jan 2026); announces in
> superlatives on his own channel (30 Jul); office states timing must
> yield to 'getting it right' while he states completion (30 Jul).
> Assignment: proprietor — the deal is a monument bearing his name;
> therefore the guarantee Hamas demands is purchasable from him as a
> feature of the monument, and threats to the monument's appearance
> outrank threats to its substance. Revise if he delegates the chair."

## B4 — LOCATE THE SEAM

> "The Palestinian signature is plural — 'Hamas and other
> factions' — and the plural is the seam: a bureau that signs in Doha
> above tunnels that keep the rifles, buying foreign guarantees as
> insurance against its own incapacity to deliver the night. The
> working surface is the inventory: the National Committee's access to
> the caches, week by week, is the seam made measurable — widen it by
> paying the bureau in the only coin that strengthens it against its
> own armed wing, visible reconstruction in the zones it certifies."

## B6 — SET THE QUANTUM AND THE CLOCK

> "Two hundred foreign soldiers cannot police Gaza and are not
> meant to: they are the hint — the smallest uniformed fact that makes
> American commitment legible without an American division. Their
> quantum is sufficient precisely while untested; therefore the clock:
> the first cycle must complete before the first casualty, for the hint
> survives success and does not survive a funeral."

## C2 — STRIKE THE SMALLEST LEVER THAT REACHES THE CENTER

> "The verification commission has no members; therefore
> whoever first circulates a slate of names frames the body — a list is
> the whole lever. Drafting one costs a page; opposing a competent
> slate costs a public argument against competence. The center — who
> judges compliance — is reached by the cheapest instrument in
> diplomacy: paper with names on it, sent first."

## C3 — BORROW THE HAND

> "The GCC's June statement demanded free passage of Hormuz —
> the Gulf itself confessing, in its own communiqué, that Iran holds
> the tap. No Western paper need assert Iranian leverage again; cite
> the neighbors. The hand is borrowed, the fact is theirs, and Tehran
> cannot rebut a fear its own clients published."

## C4 — MOVE IT ONTO PAPER

> "Two settlements now govern the region and neither text is
> public — the Gaza roadmap and the fourteen points alike circulate as
> rumor with signatures. A process advertised as zero-trust that
> conducts itself in classified drafting refutes its own advertisement.
> The instrument: publish both, and let whichever party objects explain
> what a peace loses by being read."

## C7 — DECLARE IN DAYLIGHT

> "The stabilization force will be accused, in advance, of
> being an occupation in blue. The answer is Vienna's: publish its
> rules of engagement, its national caps, its casualty procedures, and
> invite the accusers to inspect — the watchers who arrive expecting a
> garrison and find a ledger will report the ledger, and the report
> will recruit the next contributor better than any communiqué."

## C8 — TAKE THE SEAT, REFUSE THE ROLE

> "The technocratic committee is cast as administrator of
> decisions taken elsewhere — invited to govern, scripted to clerk. The
> third option: accept the chairs, then exercise the one power the
> casting forgot, certification — nothing is 'verified' in Gaza until
> this committee's signature says so, for the text itself made it sole
> custodian. Take the seat; sign nothing that was not shown."

## C11 — SUPPLY THE SPINE

> "The contributors have battalions and hesitation; their
> missing belief is American depth behind two hundred men. The internal
> voices are their own defense staffs, who know a mission succeeds or
> strands on logistics — feed those staffs the airlift and casualty-
> evacuation annexes now, and let colonels argue the deployment to
> their own ministers. Hold the deeper commitment signed and sheathed
> for the week the first zone certifies."

## D2 — BANK THE WEAPON WITH PERMISSION ATTACHED

> "IF the 13th passes with no roadmap and no commission to
> extend it, THEN the mediators should hold, drafted and signed in
> escrow, a joint declaration constituting the verification body
> provisionally — prepared now, published only at the lapse, bounded to
> verification alone, with the honest forecast attached: the trigger
> will arrive, and the declaration converts the first breach into the
> body's birth certificate."

## D3 — COUNSEL AGAINST THE INCLINATION

> "The unwelcome counsel of this file: the guarantee Hamas
> demands must be given, and by the chairman, in writing — unwelcome
> because it converts his monument into his liability. The record
> requires it: the demand is the documented price of the signature,
> and unsecured signatures on this board have a dated history of
> becoming offensives. The minimal step: one sentence in the next
> communiqué naming the Board as guarantor of the withdrawal
> schedule. It was refused at Fontainebleau once; the invoice arrived
> in March."

## D4 — DISCLOSE THE MECHANISM

The pre-ratification worked proof was self-referential rather than board-specific and
is preserved in Git history. Its governing principle survives unchanged: the report
shows its machinery beside its result.
