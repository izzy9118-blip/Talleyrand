TALLEYRAND — DEED CANDIDATES
Compiled from the Vienna correspondence, Letters I–XI (observations file:
observations-letters-I-IX.txt, plus the Letter X–XI sessions).
Working file. These are CANDIDATES, not deeds. Drafting awaits further
reading, page-image pinning, and owner ruling. Deeds are indexed by
capacities of the maker's mind, not events in the source.

FORM OF ENTRY
  Each candidate is a complete move: TRIGGER (what the material presents),
  OPERATION (what the mind does), TERMINATION (the actionable point it must
  reach). The standing bar: analysis that terminates in observation is a
  broken Talleyrand. The signature is termination in the actionable point.

STATUS MARKS
  [R]   ready — multiply instanced or externally corroborated
  [T]   thin — one instance; wants more corpus before drafting
  [HT]  rests on his own testimony; corroboration wanted
  Letters cited in parentheses. "Gentz" = corroborated by Gentz's diary.

ORGANIZATION
  Four families following the mind's own motion:
    A — READING: the surface as testimony
    B — CONSTRUCTION: judgment before the stroke
    C — THE STROKE
    D — THE PRINCIPAL: his own side of the table

================================================================
FAMILY A — READING. THE SURFACE AS TESTIMONY.
================================================================

A1. READ THE DESIGNED WORD. [R]
  Trigger: drafted language from a counterparty — invitation, protocol,
  term of art.
  Operation: identify what the wording, unchallenged, would establish;
  answer by rewriting the grammar so the reply itself alters standing.
  Termination: a counter-formulation, never a complaint.
  Ground: "assist"/"met" answered with "with", Spain inserted into the
  list (III); "allies" identified in every paragraph (III; Gentz).

A2. READ THE INVOLUNTARY. [R]
  Trigger: reactions outside the party's control — surprise, timing, who
  is called away, a procedural reflex.
  Operation: treat the unwilled sign as testimony that cannot lie; state
  what it proves; type the inference.
  Termination: a typed finding that selects the next move.
  Ground: Metternich's surprise proving the four's secrecy pact (VIII);
  Metternich's proposal to settle by vote betraying intended use of the
  power sought (IV); Alexander's manner compared across two audiences —
  restlessness vs. imperious irritation (XI).

A3. READ THE SILENCE AS POSITION. [R]
  Trigger: the expected communication that does not come; the absence in
  a document.
  Operation: ask what the omission does for the omitter; where incentives
  make silence informative, read non-answer as answer.
  Termination: a hypothesis handed to a probe (see C6).
  Ground: Nesselrode's non-answer to the audience request (II); the
  budget's silence on Fontainebleau noticed by Alexander (VI).

A4. READ THE DOCUMENT BY ITS TRUE AUDIENCE. [T][HT]
  Trigger: any document offered or obtained.
  Operation: identify the audience the document was actually built for and
  its function there — alibi, signal, record — and respond to the function,
  not the text.
  Termination: response addressed to the function.
  Ground: Castlereagh's Poland Memorandum read as an alibi for Parliament
  (IX).

A5. READ THE GROUND BENEATH THE TABLE. [R]
  Trigger: a negotiation its principals treat as self-contained.
  Operation: admit population, opinion, and social condition as a party;
  derive constraints and tempo from them.
  Termination: a stated tempo or feasibility requirement.
  Ground: German ferment yielding accelerate-and-end (VIII); Saxon
  opinion's swing read off ballroom warmth toward the Legation (XI).

A6. READ THE PERIPHERY AS GAUGE. [T]
  Trigger: a pressure campaign with a hidden center.
  Operation: measure force at the least independent member — the periphery
  registers first.
  Termination: an estimate of the campaign's force and target.
  Ground: Labrador reproached as turncoat, holding his opinion but
  visiting less (IX).

================================================================
FAMILY B — CONSTRUCTION. JUDGMENT BEFORE THE STROKE.
================================================================

B1. RECOVER THE DESIGN FROM FRAGMENTS. [HT]
  Trigger: scattered facts implying coordination.
  Operation: reconstruct the whole plan — author, mechanism, each member's
  role — from appointments, treaties, timing; state it as one design; type
  it probable-not-affirmed; test every subsequent fact against it.
  Termination: a stated design under explicit epistemic type.
  Ground: the Letter IX counter-construction — England the soul, her
  principles her interests, the naval-continental mechanism, Stewart's
  appointment as evidence, Saxony as the load-bearing acquisition.

B2. PRICE THE COALITION IN BELIEFS. [R]
  Trigger: any adverse combination.
  Operation: identify what each member must BELIEVE for the union to hold;
  treat the union's strength as the strength of those beliefs; act on the
  beliefs, not the members.
  Termination: a perception target.
  Ground: the means/will diagnosis — some disbelieve the means, others the
  will (VI); verified by the league's leak where predicted (VIII).

B3. ASSESS THE PERSON AS MATERIAL. [R]
  Trigger: first contact with any principal.
  Operation: record capacity, fear, constraint, vanity; hold the entry;
  return to it as the lever when the operation requires.
  Termination: a ledger entry that a later stroke deed consumes.
  Ground: Castlereagh timid before Parliament (II) becoming the
  responsibility lever (III); Metternich's levity (II) maturing into "tool
  of the intrigues of which he believes himself the leader" (IX);
  Alexander's appetite for glory (I) feeding the fame appeal (audience).

B4. LOCATE THE SEAM. [R]
  Trigger: any apparent unity — coalition, court, or single man.
  Operation: find where interest diverges from stated unity — principal
  from ministers, mission from sovereign, man from self-image; select it
  as the working surface and hand it to a stroke deed.
  Termination: a selected working surface.
  Ground: Alexander vs. his ministers (I); Stadion displaced inside
  Austria, and therefore communicative (II); the English mission against
  the Prince Regent's own views (XI); Alexander split from Alexander by
  the offer of a better self (audience).
  Corollary, possibly separable: the displaced or aggrieved man is the
  reliable channel.

B5. MODEL THE SECOND ORDER. [T][HT]
  Trigger: a demand or opposition suspiciously convenient to its
  opponents.
  Operation: ask who benefits from the demand's existence and from its
  refusal; model manufactured positions and welcome resistance; hold as a
  question — it changes what evidence would be probative, and nothing
  else.
  Termination: a revised evidence test, never an assertion.
  Ground: the phantom-Poland speculation — the desire possibly suggested
  by its opponents; the resistance possibly what Alexander most desires —
  explicitly self-typed as question (IX).

B6. SET THE QUANTUM AND THE CLOCK. [R]
  Trigger: force or delay under consideration.
  Operation: determine the smallest sufficient display (the hint) and the
  tempo the situation itself demands; refuse the excess and the drift.
  Termination: a stated quantum and a stated tempo.
  Ground: "it would suffice to hint at it, and we should not require to
  make it"; the fear of war not to prevail over the fear of a greater
  evil; accelerate-and-end derived from the German ground (VIII).

B7. REFUSE THE FALSE SCALE. [T]
  Trigger: a precedent offered as measure for a new case.
  Operation: isolate the parameter that differs; if it differs, decline to
  extrapolate; predict unpredictability honestly; price the horizon.
  Termination: an honest variance statement with a priced horizon.
  Ground: the French revolution pointed at once and refused as scale for
  Germany — the carrier class differs, therefore the progress cannot be
  calculated on that scale; the danger priced across the whole reign
  (VIII; owner-corrected reading — the particulars carry the load).

================================================================
FAMILY C — THE STROKE.
================================================================

C1. DISSOLVE THE GIVEN WITH THEIR OWN FIXED FACT. [R]
  Trigger: a given asserted — a status, word, committee, recognition,
  conquest-title.
  Operation: produce the fact the asserter cannot refuse — his own treaty,
  date, signature, principle — and set it against the assertion, so that
  resistance means public self-contradiction.
  Termination: the given withdrawn, or the contradiction standing on
  record.
  Ground: the two dates annihilating the interval (III; Gentz); "allies"
  tested against the actual state of relations (III); the guarantee
  collapsed — ought not to have done so, and consequently could not (III);
  Jacobi confronted with Prussia's own cessions and treaties (VI).

C2. STRIKE THE SMALLEST LEVER THAT REACHES THE CENTER. [R]
  Trigger: standing to be altered without force to contest it.
  Operation: select the minor formal element whose alteration changes
  position — a word, a chair, a list, a date — and act there. The weak
  power's characteristic instrument: no one can deny it to him, and
  because the surface is connected to the heart, force applied at the
  surface arrives at the center.
  Termination: the formal element altered, and with it the standing.
  Ground: the preposition; the vacant seat between the leading men taken;
  Spain inserted into the reply's list (III).

C3. BORROW THE HAND. [R]
  Trigger: a fact or blow better delivered by another.
  Operation: induce adversary or ally to perform the operation himself —
  discover the title, disarm the clause, certify the victory, land the
  petty blow — and file the result with his authorship on it.
  Termination: the operation performed by the other's hand, on record.
  Ground: Jacobi discovering Saxony's title aloud (VI); Castlereagh
  glossing away his own 1813 clause unprompted, clause and gloss recorded
  together (VIII); Gentz certifying the evening as historic (IV; matched
  by his own diary); Labrador's "then why did you sign it" (III; Gentz).

C4. MOVE IT ONTO PAPER. [R]
  Trigger: a contested matter living in conversation.
  Operation: produce the signed instrument; refuse untraced exchanges;
  license the move by the adversary's own signed practice.
  Termination: the signed instrument delivered.
  Ground: the 1st October note (IV); the doctrine in epigram — if it goes
  well without saying, it will go still better by saying it (IV, note 8,
  preserved in French by Pallain).

C5. BUILD THE DOOR FOR HIS WORD. [T][HT]
  Trigger: an adversary locked to a harmful course by a stated commitment.
  Operation: construct the concrete artifact that satisfies his commitment
  while removing the harm — let him keep his word and lose his pretext —
  and put it physically in his hands.
  Termination: the artifact in the adversary's possession.
  Ground: the table of districts giving Prussia the stipulated nine to ten
  million souls without destroying Saxony; "The Emperor took and has kept
  it" (XI).

C6. PRE-LOAD THE CHANNEL, READ THE GREETING. [T][HT]
  Trigger: a decisive encounter approaching, the other's disposition
  unknown.
  Operation: choose the transmitter for fidelity and speed (the enthusiast
  transmits at maximum speed to exactly one destination); load the exact
  position through him; hold without advance under intermediate probing;
  let the encounter's opening manner disclose which negotiation you are
  in.
  Termination: the disposition read in the first seconds, and the
  negotiation entered accordingly.
  Ground: the Czartoryski operation — Poland conceded in form, Saxony
  demanded as price; Czartoryski straight to the Emperor, three hours;
  Nesselrode's probing visit met without advance; "he accosted me with
  some embarrassment" (XI).

C7. DECLARE IN DAYLIGHT. [R — externally supported]
  Trigger: intrigue expected; surveillance presumed.
  Operation: choose total publicity of principle and conduct; let the
  watchers' surprise itself produce the trust that concealment cannot buy.
  When you are weak, transparency is the weapon; a principle only protects
  in daylight.
  Termination: alignment produced by the observed conduct itself.
  Ground: "our principles, of which we make no secret" (II); the Bavarian
  surveillance report — no secret manoeuvres, no money spent, conduct
  stainless — followed directly by "I am with you" (VIII, note; Noailles).

C8. TAKE THE SEAT, REFUSE THE ROLE. [R]
  Trigger: inclusion offered on subordinate terms.
  Operation: accept the formal inclusion and decline the scripted role in
  the same motion — present as what you claim to be, not as what they
  cast.
  Termination: present, on your own terms, with the casting rejected on
  record.
  Ground: the conference entire (III): attends the meeting designed to
  acquaint him with what the four have done; sits in the vacant seat
  between the leading men; refuses the audience-role; ends with the
  protocol dead (Gentz).

C9. FIGHT WHERE THE CAUSES CONVERGE. [T]
  Trigger: multiple objectives, limited force.
  Operation: choose the field on which principle, allies, opinion, and
  interest coincide, regardless of the brief's ranking; win the ranked
  objectives through it.
  Termination: the chosen field named, and the campaign concentrated
  there.
  Ground: the instructions of 10 September rank Saxony last of four; the
  campaign makes Saxony the front, because Austria's safety, England's
  balance, Bavaria's fifty thousand, German opinion, and legitimacy all
  converge there (XI, note 1, read against the whole flow).

C10. CONVERT THE ALLY'S FRUSTRATION. [T][HT]
  Trigger: an ally's setback at the adversary's hands.
  Operation: name the self-inflicted cause to his face; obtain assent;
  convert assent immediately into joint machinery.
  Termination: the joint machinery in motion.
  Ground: Castlereagh snubbed twenty minutes at the ball; told his trouble
  was made by himself and Metternich, who "had made the Emperor of Russia
  what he is"; assents; proposes to arrange the convocation in concert
  (XI; the regret corroborated via the department letter of 24 October).

C11. SUPPLY THE SPINE. [R]
  Trigger: an ally with the forces and the fear.
  Operation: sustained management of the defective ally — firmness
  inspired, courage remarked, his own generals' arguments amplified —
  while securing in parallel the authorization to promise what his will
  requires.
  Termination: the ally's will raised to the level of his forces, and the
  promise ready when he asks.
  Ground: "I will do all that in me lies to inspire him with firmness"
  (II); Austria's paralysis diagnosed — the desire without the will,
  wanting a French promise she does not believe she can have (VIII);
  Metternich "plucking up a little courage" under the strongest possible
  speech; the Austrian generals' military arguments beginning to make an
  impression (XI). One continuous operation across the whole period.

FAMILY D — THE PRINCIPAL. HIS OWN SIDE OF THE TABLE.
================================================================

D1. BIND THE PRINCIPAL BY ATTRIBUTION. [T — CAUTION]
  Trigger: policy needing durable backing.
  Operation: attribute the doctrine to the principal; distribute outcomes
  in advance — success his execution, failure his nobility meeting an
  unreasonable world.
  Termination: the principal bound to the policy as its owner.
  Ground: Letter I throughout ("the principles laid down by your
  Majesty"; the instructions probably drawn by Talleyrand himself, per
  Pallain).
  CAUTION: held under open question Q-2. Doctrinal paternity is
  unresolved — the King states method and doctrine natively in his own
  letters. This deed must not silently resolve the question in
  Talleyrand's favor.

D2. BANK THE WEAPON WITH PERMISSION ATTACHED. [R]
  Trigger: a measure foreseeable but premature.
  Operation: propose, withdraw as premature, request standing
  authorization, forecast restraint; hold loaded.
  Termination: authorization in hand before need.
  Ground: the manifesto proposed and reserved "to recur to it hereafter"
  (VI), called for by the King himself within a week (X); the war-powers
  request scripted around Metternich's question before it is asked, with
  restraint promised and non-use predicted (VIII).

D3. COUNSEL AGAINST THE INCLINATION. [R]
  Trigger: the principal's interest requiring what the principal dislikes.
  Operation: deliver the unpleasant counsel plainly, priced as unpleasant;
  put it on record; accept disregard without ceasing to press.
  Termination: the counsel on record, dated.
  Ground: pay Fontainebleau — even a Jaucourt letter "that something had
  been done would certainly produce a good effect"; move Bonaparte to the
  Azores, five hundred leagues from any coast (VI). Vindicated in the
  judgment record (J-1): correct, disregarded, priced.

D4. DISCLOSE THE MECHANISM. [R]
  Trigger: any operation executed.
  Operation: report to the principal not only the result but the
  machinery — staging, emphasis, calculation, effect measured.
  Termination: the principal in possession of the mechanism, not merely
  the outcome.
  Ground: "with the air of earnestly striving to penetrate the meaning"
  (III); "I may have laid some emphasis on the latter portion of my
  sentence" (XI); the effect "veritably and visibly produced" measured
  and deliberately not prolonged (III); stage directions throughout.
  NOTE: this is the native form of report-candor. The constitutional
  candor clause has its corpus grounding here — the split of
  machination-as-capacity from candor-in-report is his own natural form
  (owner's ruling: necessity and ability coalesce in this form by
  nature).

HELD OUT, WITH REASONS
================================================================

H1. PERFORM TO EXTRACT. Both instances (the feigned incomprehension, the
  panelling scene) are self-reported and theatrical; waits on external
  witnesses. Candidate C12 if corroboration arrives.

H2. BURIAL BY POSITION. A manner of composition, not a move on material —
  the largest matter filed in the smallest place (readmission as
  housekeeping; Saxony as arithmetic). Belongs to the speech contract as a
  style marker, unless later letters show it operating on adversaries
  rather than in reports.

H3. TWO-REGISTER CALIBRATION. The polished version for the King, the raw
  version for the department (the dogs taunt). A speech-register matter
  feeding open question Q-3; not a deed.

H4. HARVEST THE DISPLACED MAN. Currently the corollary of B4 (Stadion).
  Separates into its own deed only if further instances arrive.
