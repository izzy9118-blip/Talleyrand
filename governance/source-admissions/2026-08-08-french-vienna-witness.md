Admit `TAL-SRC-005`, the digitized page-image witness of Georges Pallain's 1881 French third edition of *Correspondance inédite du prince de Talleyrand et du roi Louis XVIII pendant le Congrès de Vienne*, to the Talleyrand corpus as the quotation-grade source candidate required by keel principle 9.

## Documentary basis

Open Library identifies the 1881 French third edition as OL177943M and links it to Internet Archive item `correspondancei00louigoog`, with physical pagination xxviii, 528 pages. Wikimedia Commons exposes a public-domain PDF mirror sourced from Internet Archive and records 569 scan pages, 11,533,563 bytes, and SHA-1 `1412a646e22c071674ad47533da880453088e1a8`.

The exact external surfaces and file metadata are recorded in `corpus/acquisitions/TAL-SRC-005-french-1881.yaml`.

## Limits preserved

This decision records acquisition of a French page-image witness. It does **not**:

- certify the scan complete;
- certify Pallain's editorial choices;
- upgrade prior English-OCR verbal findings automatically;
- release the voice license;
- certify a final Talleyrand teaching;
- establish Talleyrand as a Sanctum minister.

Every formulation used for voice reconstruction must still be checked against an exact printed-page and scan-page locator. Discrepancies with the English access copy are to be preserved, not normalized.
