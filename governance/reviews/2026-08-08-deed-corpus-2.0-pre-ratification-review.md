## Deed-specific review

### Amendment required before ratification

- **0 SEE ONE BOARD** — `Refuse the file's own frame first` conflicts with source-first reconstruction. Preserve the file's own frame as evidence while refusing its claim to exhaust the board.
- **A1 READ THE DESIGNED WORD** — rejected alternatives may reveal design only when the alternative/rejection is itself documented; otherwise the deed invites invented drafting history.
- **A2 READ THE INVOLUNTARY** — an involuntary-looking reaction is an indicator, not self-authenticating proof. Staging, misunderstanding, and rival explanations must remain live until separated by ground.
- **A3 READ THE SILENCE AS POSITION** — silence must be reconciled with the Sanctum/Horus source-absence law. Ordinary absence is unresolved; documented suppression/expected communication must be positively grounded.
- **B2 PRICE THE COALITION IN BELIEFS** — belief assignments require source/dated-conduct ground and epistemic typing. An adverse combination may be real even if its cohesion is belief-dependent; the deed may not dissolve it by assumption.
- **B3 ASSESS THE PERSON AS MATERIAL** — integrate the already-proposed capacity-pricing amendment into the deed itself; preserve person/body separation; wants, fears, and self-image are inferences from dated conduct, not free portraiture.
- **B4 LOCATE THE SEAM** — displacement does not automatically make a person communicative; every proposed seam must be evidenced and persons/bodies remain separate.
- **C3 BORROW THE HAND** — the operation may create an occasion for another actor to discover or state a fact, but may not seed, script, or manufacture the evidentiary result. Authorship/provenance must remain genuinely the other's.
- **C4 MOVE IT ONTO PAPER** — replace the obsolete `IV n.8`/modernized epigram with `TAL-VOICE-PIN-001`: Letter No. 4, body text, printed p. 35, exact French wording. Also preserve the distinction between conversation, formal note, declaration, and signed instrument.
- **C11 SUPPLY THE SPINE** — avoid a one-way causal story parallel to retired C10. Internal voices may be relied upon only when documented; the minister cannot manufacture a faction merely because it would be useful.
