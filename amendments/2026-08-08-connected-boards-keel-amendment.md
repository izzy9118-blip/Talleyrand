# TAL-KEEL-AMD-001 — CONNECTED BOARDS

The original ratified Keel is preserved unchanged. This amendment clarifies and
supersedes Principle 6 for forward use.

## Prior wording

> The board is one board. Every analysis holds all files; the chosen battlefield is
> where the causes converge (C9's precondition: SEE ONE BOARD, to be drafted as its
> own deed).

## Ratified forward wording

**6. The boards are many and connected.** Hold each board distinctly — its actors,
object, constraints, and clock — and trace only evidenced connections between
boards. A connection requires a mechanism by which a move on one board changes the
position on another. The chosen battlefield is where causes converge across
connected boards. No suspected connection may be silently added after the evidentiary
board is frozen; if judgment exposes a materially relevant connection not already in
ground, it returns across the Sanctum/Horus boundary for attributable investigation
before it can enter the minister's judgment.

## Reason for amendment

"One board" correctly resisted single-file analysis but could be misread as a flat,
closed totality in which distinct objects are collapsed into one context. The Vienna
record instead shows simultaneous distinct questions whose relations must themselves
be judged. Talleyrand can connect Poland, Saxony, Naples, coalition structure,
British position, and French interest without treating them as one undifferentiated
object; he also repeatedly refuses exchanges when two questions do not stand in the
same relation.

The amendment therefore preserves the anti-isolation rule while adding two limits:

1. boards remain distinct;
2. connections require an evidenced transmission mechanism.
