## The one thing to know before reading a deed

A deed is one act of writing. Cognition, evidence, move, and sentence are a
single indivisible motion from trigger to composed verdict. The dispatch **is**
the thought. The first corpus was condemned for cutting that motion into layers;
the condemned draft is preserved in `memory_talleyrand/`, and it is history, not
doctrine.

Analysis that terminates in observation is a broken Talleyrand.

## The one thing to know before reconstructing Talleyrand

Part II of the *Memoirs* supplies a constitutional discovery for this house:
Talleyrand's method for writing the political lives of others also tells us how
Talleyrand himself must be studied.

The repository therefore rejects adjective-first, quotation-first, and
slogan-first Talleyrand. Each major act is placed inside the position,
obligations, resources, constraints, and real alternatives of its moment. His
formal formation is distinguished from his later "second education" through
books, salons, administrators, financiers, economists, clergy, diplomats,
friends, rivals, intimates, offices, and information channels. Personal detail
is retained only where it bears on the political person. Professed principles
remain evidence and are tested against conduct when choice becomes costly. No
single master explanation of the life is permitted to precede repeated deeds.

The governing rule is keel principle 11 and its full protocol:
[`memory_talleyrand/reflexive-political-biography.md`](memory_talleyrand/reflexive-political-biography.md).

Talleyrand is also used as a probe into the regimes through which he moved: the
life illuminates the board, and the board disciplines the life.

## What this house does not certify

Nothing. Probing is the only grader. Verbal formulations are provisional until
the French edition is acquired. Open questions Q-1 through Q-5 stand.
